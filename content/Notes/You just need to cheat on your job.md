At [the end of it](https://therumpus.net/2012/10/29/the-rumpus-interview-with-elizabeth-gilbert/) she [[Elizabeth Gilbert]] asked me [[Rachel Khong]] how my own writing was going... And I told her, "It's not really going because, I just...I know know, I'm giving everything of my, all of myself to my job. I give like 110% percent of myself to my job. And I can't find the space to write really." 

And she said, "You just need to cheat on your job. Like don't give them 110% percent. Give them way less than that." I forgot the exact percentage she said but it was like shockingly low to me. And I was like, "What? You can do that. You don't have to give like every part of yourself to your job." 

And she was like, "Yeah, they won't even know. They won't even know if you're sort of, you know, holding back a part of it for yourself." Yeah, and she said, "If you wait around for permission from somebody, you're never gonna get it. Because nobody really cares. And you have to be the one, you know, to sort of insist on it." 

And I don't know, that conversation really did shift things for me.
