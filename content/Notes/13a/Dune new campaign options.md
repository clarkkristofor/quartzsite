# New Campaign Options

<aside>
<img src="https://cdn-icons-png.flaticon.com/512/3564/3564180.png" alt="https://cdn-icons-png.flaticon.com/512/3564/3564180.png" width="40px" /> **Murk Wardens**

The heroes are Murk Wardens, technicians of the magical wards that stabilize and protect the [Archmage](../Websites%2034982b842540426082b33f4dd243a5f7/super%20clark%20113015ce4815434ba7c0fcd1db149ea7/Places%20detail%206e7af666d83847c6a95550053012d0ba/Dune-inspired%20Campaign%20Player%20Handout%20461cc1dc80b54b3585777ac0d93093ad.md)’s lucrative murkship lines. You are based in Sikara’s [Murkport](../Websites%2034982b842540426082b33f4dd243a5f7/super%20clark%20113015ce4815434ba7c0fcd1db149ea7/Places%20detail%206e7af666d83847c6a95550053012d0ba/Sikara%20Murkport%20e00279f281424ce9abf859d2a482304b.md). Your squad’s wards are failing, and it's your fault. So far you’ve managed to hide your mistakes from your superiors, while you work frantically on a solution. But time is running out. The critical phase wards couldn’t *completely* collapse, could they?

- good for wizards and other arcane casters and their muscle
- potential for a sillier, more slapstick campaign, but not necessarily
</aside>

<aside>
<img src="https://cdn-icons-png.flaticon.com/512/3564/3564186.png" alt="https://cdn-icons-png.flaticon.com/512/3564/3564186.png" width="40px" /> **Clan Flamekeeper**

The heroes are elders and trusted allies of the priestly orc Clan Flamekeeper at a time of existential crisis. A brutal band of [House Abulyrd](../Websites%2034982b842540426082b33f4dd243a5f7/super%20clark%20113015ce4815434ba7c0fcd1db149ea7/Places%20detail%206e7af666d83847c6a95550053012d0ba/Dune-inspired%20Campaign%20Player%20Handout%20461cc1dc80b54b3585777ac0d93093ad.md) insurgents invaded the icy wastes the Clan calls home. The splinter Clan Rage Claw, led by Angar the She-Shaman and her demonic cult, is dividing and harassing clans loyal to the [Orc Lord](../Websites%2034982b842540426082b33f4dd243a5f7/super%20clark%20113015ce4815434ba7c0fcd1db149ea7/Places%20detail%206e7af666d83847c6a95550053012d0ba/Dune-inspired%20Campaign%20Player%20Handout%20461cc1dc80b54b3585777ac0d93093ad.md). And the sacred flame is dying. Can you reignite the flame, protect the Clan, and hold the Orc Lord’s clans together?

- most characters must be orcs or half-orcs
- expect to fight bad guys, perform sacred rituals for all orc clans loyal to the [Orc Lord](../Websites%2034982b842540426082b33f4dd243a5f7/super%20clark%20113015ce4815434ba7c0fcd1db149ea7/Places%20detail%206e7af666d83847c6a95550053012d0ba/Dune-inspired%20Campaign%20Player%20Handout%20461cc1dc80b54b3585777ac0d93093ad.md), uncover the mystery of the dying flame, all while leading the clan
</aside>

<aside>
<img src="https://cdn-icons-png.flaticon.com/512/3564/3564189.png" alt="https://cdn-icons-png.flaticon.com/512/3564/3564189.png" width="40px" /> **Courtiers of Zilharr the Radiant**

The heroes are members of the ragtag court of Zilharr the Radiant, a great silver dragon loyal to the [Great Gold Wyrm](../Websites%2034982b842540426082b33f4dd243a5f7/super%20clark%20113015ce4815434ba7c0fcd1db149ea7/Places%20detail%206e7af666d83847c6a95550053012d0ba/Dune-inspired%20Campaign%20Player%20Handout%20461cc1dc80b54b3585777ac0d93093ad.md). Zilharr’s eyrie sits high above Sikara. Your mission is a delicate and urgent one: rally support for [House Ravd](../Websites%2034982b842540426082b33f4dd243a5f7/super%20clark%20113015ce4815434ba7c0fcd1db149ea7/Places%20detail%206e7af666d83847c6a95550053012d0ba/Dune-inspired%20Campaign%20Player%20Handout%20461cc1dc80b54b3585777ac0d93093ad.md) among the minor houses and great powers, while quietly fomenting rebellion among the imperial dragons—mounts of the Knights of the Noonday Sun, source of the [Emperor](../Websites%2034982b842540426082b33f4dd243a5f7/super%20clark%20113015ce4815434ba7c0fcd1db149ea7/Places%20detail%206e7af666d83847c6a95550053012d0ba/Dune-inspired%20Campaign%20Player%20Handout%20461cc1dc80b54b3585777ac0d93093ad.md)’s fearsome power. Can you do it before Ravd is destroyed?

- full of diplomacy, political intrigue, world travel, and dragons!
- play underdog good guys trying to pull off a miracle
</aside>

<aside>
<img src="https://cdn-icons-png.flaticon.com/512/1828/1828078.png" alt="https://cdn-icons-png.flaticon.com/512/1828/1828078.png" width="40px" /> **a Minor House’s gamble**

The heroes are operatives from one of two minor houses (see below). Generations of corruption and incompetence have brought the house to the edge of financial ruin. But fortune favors the bold, and if the rumor is true and if you can make savvy moves amidst the chaos and conflict in Sikara, you and your house will rise dramatically in your icon’s favor.

- opportunism, the thrill of the wager, and/or fear of poverty and shame drive your superiors, more than morals or grand ideologies
- pursue the rumor that drew you to Sikara:
