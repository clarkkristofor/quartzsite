# Oar

*small fishing village (and pilgrim way) near the Purge Lands*

- Gulrymsul stole the Lightning Witch's soul. Uncle Obran failed to retrieve it from the Floating Plateau. The Pit. (Where the last taproot is.)
- A wind sprite follows Gravel, causing mischief.
- A Stonehearted Knight and a Grace are in town, preaching peace to the locals. The monsignor at the Chapel was uninterested.
- There's a pub but no inn in Oar proper. It's all fisher-people who have houses. Secondary industry. And a few miles from town, the Purge Lands.
- The Blue Willy is what non-religious locals call the inn most pilgrims stay at, outskirts, nearest the Chapel of Saint's Rest on the Purge Lands. Sky blue, paint peeling. Three story central tower with one story wings.
- The Chapel is in devils' hands. They're using this weak point to pull Gulrymsul through.
- NPCs
    - *Fishermen:* Galen, Arlen, Marlen; Orlan, Sully, Crane, Jinx
    - *Pub:* Hannity and Cipra, children Lee, "Lady," and Ophelia
    - *Monsignor Crockett (human):* gruff but generous, disarming but keen to hide the devils' work to keep his daughter well - appointed by the Emperox to oversee the Purge Lands
    - *Chaplain-Adjutant Arlena (half elf):* uptight and self-righteous but also actually righteous and paranoid about plot she dimly perceives - oversees rituals and wards to heal the hell rift, spies for Varda
        - Suspicious.
            - A handful of years ago, a surprising turnover in nun/monks
            - A modest increase in pilgrims. With a surge of entreaties about family gone missing on pilgrimage.
        - The rift has been healing faster lately. Last couple years. It looks different from the rest.
    - *Brother Marlen, Minister of Hospitality (human):* dim but lucky - leads the ministries to the pilgrims
    - *Karalon family (human, mostly):* Helen (grandmother/mother), Bruce (father/son), Ada (eldest (grand)daughter), Mert (Ada's husband), Jot and Nip (twins of Ada and Mert), Aiden (middle son of Bruce) and Simeon (youngest son of Bruce) - pilgrims, sons protected them on way, youngest is compromised (very strong, eye symbol)
