# Event Focus Table

1-7 . . . . . . . .News comes of a remote event
8-28 . . . . . . .NPC makes a surprise action
29-35 . . . . . .Introduce a new NPC
36-45 . . . . . .Achieving a goal gets easier
46-52 . . . . . .Achieving a goal gets harder
53-55 . . . . . .A goal is achieved
56-67 . . . . . .PC negative
68-75 . . . . . .PC positive
76-83 . . . . . .Ambiguous event
84-92 . . . . . .NPC negative
93-100 . . . . .NPC positive

**PC/NPC Negative or Positive:** Something bad, or good, happens to a player character, or non-player character, whichever is indicated on the event focus table.
