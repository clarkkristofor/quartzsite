### Colville's questions to players
- Who would hunt you down and kill you?
- Who would be heartbroken?
- What would you betray the company for?
Contextualize the question, so they get it. It's not about screwing you; it's about creating drama. Your character is not complete when character creation is done. Your job is to discover and follow an arc.

**GM Lessons I've Learned**
- Maximize fun, not difficulty. Make all battles 1 to 1.5 strength, except major boss fights.
- Make dramatic the consequence of failure: real stakes for characters, story consequences. Set the context.
- Test the players: present roleplaying Rorschachs and see what they do. Force a choice, instigate character-defining moments.
- Say what they see. Don't interpret. Let them figure it out themselves. But if they don't figure it out, have an NPC tell them.

**NPCs**
1. What do they want?
2. How do they express themselves?
3. How do they perceive the heroes?
