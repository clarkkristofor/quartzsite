### Archmage
- arcane training
- College of Wizardry and its many campuses, field stations, observation centers, etc.
- esteem among the masses
- flying, homunculi spying for you
### Diabolist
- infernal training (or anti-infernal training)
- far-flung secret cult (or cult-hunters)
- fear among the masses (or gratitude)
- impish minions
### Dwarf King
- dwarven training
- dwarf trade network on the land
- dwarf military network in the underworld
- curiosity among the masses
### Elf Queen
- training in secret elven arts
- mystic connection to elves
- elven scholars, artists, and expats
- human scholars in the elven tradition
- spirit servitors
- idolization among the masses
### Priestess
- holy training
- divine connection with her faithful followers
- adoration among the masses
### Emperor
- military or legal training
- sworn connection to his subjects
- imperial institutions
- the military
- official decrees
- imperial arcane technology
- deference among the masses
### Crusader
- anti-demon training
- unholy connection to his followers
- far-flung recruiting network
- far-flung spy network
- paramilitary forces
- power of the Dark Gods
- evil spirits
- dread among the masses
### High Druid
- druidic training
- natural connection to her followers
- far-flung network of druids
- nature magic
- elemental spirits and intelligent animals
- mixed feelings among the masses
### Orc Lord
- ancient power
- spies and observers
- buried magic only the Orc Lord remembers
- spirits of the horde
### Lich King
- necromantic training (or anti-necromantic)
- knowledge of ancient things
- spooky connection to his followers
- farflung network of undead (or of undead-hunters)
- phantom spirits
- terror among the masses (or gratitude)
### Great Gold Wyrm
- elite training of body and soul
- mystic connection to his devoted followers
- farflung network of paladins and their supporters
- admiration among the masses
### Prince of Shadows
- exclusive criminal training (or anti-criminal)
- uncanny connection to chosen followers
- people operating on the Prince’s orders without knowing it themselves
- far-flung network of thieves (or the Prince’s many enemies)
- admiration among the masses
### the Three
- draconic training (or anti-draconic)
- monstrous connection to a few human followers
- a small but capable underground network of draconic minions
- fear among the masses
