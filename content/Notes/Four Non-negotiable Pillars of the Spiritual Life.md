Looking at this, we see that Jesus was prescribing four things as an essential praxis for a healthy spiritual life:
a) Private prayer and private morality;
b) social justice;
c) mellowness of heart and spirit; and
d) community as a constitutive element of true worship.

Five Baptismal Promises
1. Live among God's faithful people
2. Hear God's Word & Share God's meal
3. Proclaim the good news of God in word and deed
4. Serve all people, following Christ's example
5. Strive for justice and peace in all the earth
