#### **Purpose**
**Listening**
Like the last listening campaign, this one launched on Easter Sunday and continuing into mid-May will:
- connect 1-1 with all 150+ Zion people, both members and active non-members
- build relationships of ongoing care and concern
- foster a greater sense of belonging and investment
- create clarity for mission
- develop and identify leaders
It's about strengthening Zion from the foundation--relationships.

**Synod Dean**
As of January, I am one of six Synod Deans who "work directly with the Bishop and Director for Evangelical Mission sharing in strategic conversations related to supporting congregational vitality, imagining new mission starts including ethnic specific ministries, exploring ecumenical partnerships, expanding and enhancing cross cultural/multi-cultural ministry, enhancing advocacy and community organizing focused on striving for justice and peace." I said yes to the Bishop's invitation because I have wisdom and experience to share and because seeing the broader church from this position will make me a better pastor for Zion.
