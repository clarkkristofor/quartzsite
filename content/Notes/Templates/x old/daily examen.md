# Examen Journal
A training ground for noticing and for prayerful attentiveness.
## Centering
- Take 5 minutes to center. Find your feet, find your seat, find your breath and heartbeat.
- Describe God's presence today: What image, phrase, or sensation does this evoke?
## Examen
- What energized me today?
- What drained me?
- What relationships, interactions, or moments brought joy and connection?
## Values & Character Review
**Characters of God:**
- *peace*: How did I embody peace today?
- *prayer*: Did I prioritize prayer?
- *justice*: What called for justice and compassion today?

**Strengths:** Strategic, Deliberative, Restorative, Achiever, Competitive
**Values:** contemplative courage, compassion, and creativity
- Any new insights or shifts?
- How did these guide me today?
- Did anything block me from being who I am?

**Invitation:** "Be still and know that I am God"
