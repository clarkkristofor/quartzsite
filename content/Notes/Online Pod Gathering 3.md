## Nurture
How do I care for this? Sensation, distress, emotion. Sitting in compassion, almost empathy for our own selves. Not forcing release from distress but creating conditions for release. Not trying to change anything. Don't assume it will change. Not aiming for perfection. "Progress over perfection." Little steps to knowing and loving ourselves a little better.
- In this moment what supports relief?
- What eternal conditions will allow me to find relief?
- What's at risk if I let go, soften and open?
- What supports my freedom?
Ask, relax and open. Take a nap. Drink a glass of water.

- We're safe.
- I forgive you.
- I love you.
- Or just invite heart to rest.
