## human perfection is the inclusion of imperfection
If there is such a thing as human perfection, it seems to emerge precisely from how we handle the imperfection that is everywhere, especially our own. What a clever place for God to hide holiness, so that only the humble and earnest will find it! A ‘‘perfect’’ person ends up being one who can consciously forgive and include imperfection rather than one who thinks he or she is totally above and beyond imperfection. It becomes rather obvious once you say it out loud. In fact, I would say that *the demand for the perfect is the greatest enemy of the good*. Perfection is a mathematical or divine concept, while goodness is a beautiful human concept that includes us all.
