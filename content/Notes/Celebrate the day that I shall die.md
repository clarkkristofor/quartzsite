Celebrate the day that I shall die  
and leave behind the world I’ve known from birth  
I loved the trees, the sea, the sky,  
the green enveloping this planet earth,  
the yearly hopeful spring  
the splendor that the autumns bring.  
But now rejoice with me!  
For now my soul has taken wings  
and has a peace  
that lies beyond all earthly things.

Lisa von der Lieth Zosulis
