I have made 2 tables to roll on to randomly generate supervillains, based on my observations of trends in the Deck of Villainy book for Masks: A New Generation. I've bolded every other one for easy readability. here they are!

1. **family/creator(/s)/home (protect or ruin)**
2. gaining power/wealth/influence
3. **maintaining power/wealth/influence**
4. returning to/maintaining "normality"
5. **hedonism**
6. keeping something alive/functional (self, treasured item, etc.)
7. **destruction (reality, humanity, government, etc.)**
8. targeted revenge
9. **entering a desired world (ours, a future, an alien planet, etc.)**
10. love (romantic interest/partner, an audience, etc.)

## powers/abilities (roll 1d20 up to three times):

1. **elemental (water, air, electricity, etc.)**
2. summoning (human underlings, demons, animals)
3. **superhuman toughness/strength/durability**
4. life-stealing/supernatural healing/poison
5. **mind-control/hypnosis/illusion**
6. tech/gadgets/machinery
7. **shapeshifting (partial, full, or extreme)**
8. monstrous/nonhuman form (robot, alien, undead, etc.)
9. **great wealth**
10. charm/social control
11. **intelligence/smarts/information**
12. teleportation/incorporeality
13. **giant size/tiny size/size shifting**
14. possession
15. **immobilisation (freezing, paralysing, trapping)**
16. invisibility
17. **reality control/gravity control/wormholes**
18. superhuman speed/agility/acrobatics
19. **clones/doubles**
20. weapons

**go nuts on the interpretations**, and if you want to share what you've generated in the tags/replies/comments of this post then go for it! also, if you want to make your own posts with art/stories of villains generated with this table, feel free to @ me!
