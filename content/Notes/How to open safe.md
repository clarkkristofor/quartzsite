How to open safe

1. Turn dial to the right, passing number 42 four times and stopping at that number the fifth time.
2. Turn dial to the left, passing number 80 two times and stopping of that number the third time around.
3. Turn dial to the right, passing number 64 one time and stopping at that number the second time around.
4. Turn dial to the left, to number 09 and stop.
5. ~~Insert the key in the cylindrical lock and turn it to the right to open.~~  
   We have no key. It is set to the unlocked position. Simply open the safe after set #4.
