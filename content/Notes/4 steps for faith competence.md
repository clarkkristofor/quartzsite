four steps/things that have to happen for people to know, articulate and feel competent in their faith. The four are:

1. Theology Matters - how do we help people know what the faith is?
2. If you (the leader) don’t say it, they won’t know it
3. If they (the people) don’t say it, they don’t know it (Their words matter)
4. Helping people make choices that matter (Their deeds matter)
