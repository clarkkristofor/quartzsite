Don't worry about having answers to all of these questions by the time we meet next. The first music question is the only must answer, because then I will know which of Zion's musicians to put you in touch with.

# Get to know you:

- How did you meet?
- What attracted you to each other?
- What’s the hardest challenge you’ve overcome together?
- Why get married?

# Wedding itself:

- Time and date of the wedding
- Time and date of the rehearsal
- Locations wedding, rehearsal, (and reception)

## **Families and Wedding Party**

- List of parents' names (and their spouses), siblings (if any), and the names and relationships to you of the people in your wedding party

## **Music:**

- Do you want more contemporary (keyboard) or more traditional (piano/organ)?
- Do you want the congregation to sing? A soloist? Special instrumental music? None of the above?

## **The Service:**

- Readings
- Vows
    - _**OPTION A:**_ I take you, _name_, to be my _wife/husband_ from this day forward, to join with you and share all that is to come, and I promise to be faithful to you until death parts us.
    - _**OPTION B:**_ In the presence of God and this community, I, _name_, take you, _name_, to be my _wife/husband_; to have and to hold from this day forward, in joy and in sorrow, in plenty and in want, in sickness and in health, to love and to cherish, as long as we both shall live. This is my solemn vow.
    - _**OPTION C:**_ Provide your own. Must include promise of faithfulness for the rest of your lives.
- Symbols of marriage other than rings?

# Pre-marriage Counseling

- Where do you want to focus?
- What’s strongest about your relationship?
- What do you want to make stronger?
- What’s your relationship like with your own family of origin? Your fiance’s?
