Let's find 8+ hours to play *[[Arcs]] Blighted Reach* (ABR) campaign. 
> ### [Scheduling poll](https://doodle.com/meeting/participate/id/aOqpm8Qd)
### Run Time: 12+ hours
The Arcs base game unfolds over 5 chapters. ABR unfolds over 3 "Acts"/games of 10 total chapters. The first campaign may take **12 hours for 3 players**, more for 4. It's *Twilight Imperium* sized. We may not finish in the first day-long session. That's okay.
### Starting after 8:30am
I'm free to start anytime after school drop off, roughly 8:30am.
### More RPG, less TI
If the base game is like a **tactical space game**, ABR is like a **competitive roleplaying game**. It's in conversation with *TI*, but it's ***not TI***.
### Preparing
Some pre-game rules learning will help.
- [Leder resources](https://ledergames.com/collections/games/products/arcs-the-blighted-reach-campaign-expansion)
- [SU&SD review](https://youtu.be/_GUatRy1LRk?si=V9AD9pctawd5KoTt)
- [BGG: what to expect from your first game](https://boardgamegeek.com/thread/3361067/the-blighted-reach-what-to-expect-from-your-first)
- [BGG: low-key campaign strategy guide](https://boardgamegeek.com/thread/3223608/a-low-key-guide-to-winning-campaign-arcs)

But playing the base game is the best preparation.

SU&SD recommends playing ***3 games*** of the base game before ABR. (The rules say just 1.) If you don't have 3 under your belt, we can maybe squeeze a game or two in before the full ABR day.
