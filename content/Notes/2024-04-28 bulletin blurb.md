**Tanzania connections**
By supporting Argrow's House, you also support the people of Tanzania.

Argrow's House partners with Empower Tanzania, an organization based here in Iowa and in Same ("Sah May") Town, Tanzania. Empower Tanzania serves the region near Kirangare, and Argrow's House sells items made from fabric from Empower Tanzania. 

Local Empower Tanzania people, Todd Byerly and Leslie Klipsch, helped Pastor Clark and Zion's travelers prepare for our trips. Todd and Leslie are members of St. Paul Lutheran Church in Davenport. Todd was once a missionary based in Same Town. He was there and met with travelers to Kirangare in Same Town during Zion's first trips in 2004 and 2007.

Same Town hosts the offices of Pare Diocese, Kirangare's diocese, and the Amani Hostel, where Kirangare travelers stay overnight in the trip from Kilimanjaro airport to Kirangare. During our trip in 2022, Joseph and Pastor Clark visited Empower Tanzania in Same Town. Part of the Kirangare Thank Offering in 2022 went to support Empower Tanzania too.
