## notes on meeting
### options
- TPC has no hourly or project based planning: it's all or nothing
- Andrew would have no hard feelings if we decided to go DIY and would be willing to meet informally pro bono, out to coffee once a year sort of thing
- for DIYers, finding providers to do one time assessments is hard, it's a hard business model (although we do have access to this via Portico)
### value proposition for staying with TPC (Andrew's pitch)
- TPC charges differently from others (which are commission-based)
- TPC does a blend of "assets under management" and "flat fee retainer" pricing models, reflecting a higher ethical standard and avoiding traditional conflicts of interest (wealth management/financial planning is only profession paid in medium they work it)
- **0.5% of net worth, plus flat fee** is how they got to $7,000 for us for the new service agreement
- TPC feels they provide better value even compared to others with "assets under management" and "flat fee retainer" pricing models
- the market average is 1-1.25% of "assets under management" for wealth management alone but TPC bundles other services like financial planning for less than peers
- it's true, in the first 1-2 years clients get the most value as they triage, like we've done, and get the house in order, but clients are often surprised what moves and changes in 3-4 years
- recent examples for us: interest rates went up making MIF CD and Flourish worthwhile, Portico offering a Roth option
- the TPC's proactive approach deals with things as they come up so we won't have to triage again in 4-6 years
- for us, it's a bit tricky because we have Portico accounts that have to stay with Portico, managed by Portico, not by TPC (even though the "formula" for our Portico investments is from Andrew)
- the research says people more likely to achieve financial goals working with advisors than not
- TPC will keep fee set for 3 years, unless major inheritance or influx of money, and TPC has no major plans for other-than-cost-of-living-adjustment increases in next 6 years
