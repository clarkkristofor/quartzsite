One shift might be our sense of ourselves—created as we are in God’s image. The creation story most of us have in our heads is this: God made the man Adam out of dirt, then made the woman Eve out of Adam’s rib. But that’s not quite faithful to the original Hebrew word adama, which is a non-binary word, not masculine or male. It’s more faithfully translated, “earth creature” or simply “humanity.”

Genesis 2 says, “then the Lord God formed adama from the dust of the ground and breathed into his nostrils the breath of life, and the adama became a living being.”

Male and female  God pulls the rib from Humanity’s side.
