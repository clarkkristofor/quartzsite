## promo: Listen with Courage - Jazz Music as a Metaphor for Servant Leadership 

Ailabouni will lead a workshop entitled “Listen with Courage: Jazz Music as a Metaphor for Servant Leadership”. From Congo Square in antebellum New Orleans to the music of Duke Ellington, Mary Lou Williams, and John Coltrane, jazz music has a long history of being explicitly tied to worship and spirituality. This workshop will explore how jazz carries meaning through sound and how this music offers a model for listening courageously and using our gifts to serve one another.

Ailabouni says, "Part of my call as a worship leader is to bring the joy, sophistication, and deep expressions of humanity carried by the jazz tradition and combine it with the spiritual traditions I grew up with in the Lutheran church. My goal is to invigorate, challenge, and bring new life to worship in all contexts and to support the life of congregations and synods through my work. I am particularly interested in how music can provide an entrypoint into racial justice and other social justice ministries." "Jazz is a music that carries deep spiritual meaning. The music moves the body, the mind and the Spirit. Through improvisation, musicians simultaneously draw on tradition, explore the unknown, and create new ways of being in the immediate moment. Take it from my students, improvisation can be scary! But the good news is that we aren't alone in the unknown. God is with us. And we are made to support and uplift one another. Jazz musicians sound better when each individual strives to make the others around them sound better. Similarly, we are called to servant leadership within our Christian communities."

"I am hopeful that bringing jazz into worship can be invigorating, challenging, and lifegiving for congregations, particularly in our context here in Southeastern Iowa and broadly in the ELCA."
