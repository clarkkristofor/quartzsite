## Work
Provides legal help for low income Iowans, and those over 60 in civil matters, not criminal (in criminal, they have the right to court appointed counsel).
- **Tenants facing eviction, substandard housing** - every Tuesday afternoon at Scott Co. Courthouse, 60 or 70 a week (stable and high, over 50% of their work), advice before they get to eviction, if they're having trouble paying rent; conditions of apartment; homeowners with difficulty with mortgage; buying home on contract (no "rent to own") - dangerous for people because if they miss one month, they can get evicted and lose all previous payments
	- *Call early, don't wait until eviction is scheduled. Better to prevent eviction!*
- **Family law** - mostly help for victims of domestic violence and sexual assault victims (restraining orders, elderly abuse restriction orders), some advice for divorce and custody cases
- **Consumer and other** - debt, debt collection, those being sued because of debt, protecting against wage garnishment; unemployment; medical overpayment
- **Civil Rights Violations** - sometimes refer to Civil Rights
- **Hotline for Older Iowans** - partnership with Area Agencies on Aging (Milestones, locally), navigating Medicaid, affording nursing homes; scams, ID theft; 60-70 calls per day! similar issues as above too, but also benefits related issues - **800-992-8161** - *leave message, 9-noon, 1-4pm M-F* - triage
- **Derecho** funds/needs, contractor issues, fraud, disaster preparedness (insurance)
There's a shortage of attorneys in the Davenport area. Over-the-phone legal support. May not have capacity to handle case in person but try to help as many as possible.

## Landlords
All kinds of landlords. Some are good and maintain property, etc. but these apartments are reserved for those without problems in rental history, no kids, no evictions. But those with criminal records, low income, non-white, evictions on record slide down to not so good to very bad landlords. More homeless people.
