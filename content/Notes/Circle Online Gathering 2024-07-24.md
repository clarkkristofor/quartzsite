## Allow
to let the moment, the situation, the sensation be without trying to make it different than what is is
not saying experience is okay, because it exists
a wanting that drives impulses
neither permanent nor personal

### how to back off so we don't retraumatize ourselves
focus attention on comfortable place, person
sit in meditation or prayer
get body moving with walking meditation or workout
reach out to trusted friend or professional

"This is hard. Let me be with it."
"Yes this too."
"Yes, I'm afraid/angry/sad. Let me aloow this."
"No wonder I'm X."
"This is how it is right now."
"I'm having a hard time and I care about it."
"This feels like this now. What if it doesn't always feel this way? What if healing comes?"
