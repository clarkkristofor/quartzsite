[[Barbara Holmes]] [[Living School]]

"First, let go of your preconceived notions about what is, to allow that crowded cosmos to invade every essence of your being, and to do that through prayer, meditation, music, art. Some of these things can't be expressed in words, and most of them are even beyond practice. **Practice is a tool that you use so you can get to this space where you don't need to practice anymore**, because this is an ethereal space that you can't describe, and it doesn't have rules in ways to enter or to exit. It's a safe space, but it is a space that requires a lot of us."
