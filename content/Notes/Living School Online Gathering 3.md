## How do I know I'm doing God's will and not my own?
## What is God's will? What's not? How do you know?

[[James Finley]] You don't, but a little sincerity goes a long, long way. God's infinitely in love with you in your confusion. God's will for us is God. All things considered, what's the most loving thing I can do--for self, others, plant, animal? Most powerful moment: what God meets us in a huge mistake.

> My Lord God, I have no idea where I am going. I do not see the road ahead of me. I cannot know for certain where it will end. Nor do I really know myself and the fact that I think I’m following your will does not mean that I’m actually doing so. But I believe that the desire to please you does in fact please you. And I hope I have that desire in all that I am doing. I hope that I will never do anything apart from that desire. And I know that if I do this you will lead me by the right road though I may know nothing about it. Therefore, I will trust you always. Though I may seem to be lost and in the shadow of death, I will not fear, for you are ever with me and you will never leave me to face my perils alone.

## How do you translate the word "God" to embrace all it can mean?

[[Brian McLaren]] Not contemporary vs traditional or who do we ordain but what do we mean when we say God? This invites us to go back and look at the bible a different way. Every metaphor for God was used in a certain context, with a certain set of meanings. They used metaphors that made sense to them in their context.
