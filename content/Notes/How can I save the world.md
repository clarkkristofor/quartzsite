How can I save the world?
By the grace of God, one person at a time.
How can I save the whole world one person at a time, starting with
myself? 
Because I can't offer what I don't have. 

How can I save myself, unless I accept that I'm powerless to save myself? I have to keep surrendering over to the grace of God that sustains me in my inability to sustain myself and graces me to act according to the impulses of my heart and inclinations and help people to do the same.

We're just faithful to the situation that we're in and that creates the context in which we're living this out. Whether you're a pastor of a huge congregation or you're widowed and living all alone, it's the same thing.
