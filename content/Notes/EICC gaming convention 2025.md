
## _Masks: A New Generation_ RPG
A giant robot is smashing down Main Street. Your best friend tried to kiss you. Your mom thinks your grades need work. Your mentor thinks your team is bringing you down. Oh, and your costume is ripped.  
  
Just another day in Chicago--Windy City, City of Big Shoulders, City of Supers...
  
_Masks: A New Generation_ is a superhero roleplaying game in which a team of young heroes fights villains, saves lives, and tries to figure out who they are—noble paragons? Dark avengers? Or regular kids? All against the backdrop of Chicago, the greatest city in the world.

Maximum players: 5
Experience needed: none
Materials needed: a pencil with an eraser and 2 six-sided dice will be provided to anyone who doesn't have these
Characters needed: we'll create characters together
