[[Living School]] [[Spiritual Autobiography questions]]

Pick a point in the future. It could be 1 year, 3 years, 20 years, or perhaps the memorial service held in your honor. Then use (or adapt) any of these prompts to "write your history in advance." 

- In 2024-2025, Clark was a student in the Living School's Essentials of Engaged Contemplation Course, part of the Center for Action and Contemplation. That experience changed Clark in the following ways. 
    
- People in Clark's family and close circle of friends noticed the following changes -- some subtle, some more dramatic -- in Clark since going on this learning journey. 
    
- Here is what Clark did to continue his/her/their learning journey. 
    
- Here is how this experience changed Clark's behavior in the following areas: 
    
    - self-regulation 
        
    - resolving interpersonal conflicts 
        
    - work/rest balance 
        
    - behavior toward "the other" (Define "the other" for you) 
        
    - involvement in causes or movements for change in the world, locally, regionally, nationally, and/or globally 
        
    - daily activities - buying, consuming, eating, transportation, accessing information, work and work behavior, treatment of family, strangers, friends, colleagues, customers, etc. 
        
    - involvement in a faith/spiritual/religious community 
        
    - hobbies/interests/free time activities 
        
    - voting/political involvement 
        
    - staying in touch with your circle group, cohort, and the CAC 
        
    - other
