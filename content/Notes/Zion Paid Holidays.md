Zion recognizes the following as paid holidays for all full-time and regular part-time staff:

- New Year’s Day
- Martin Luther King Jr. Day
- Easter (Monday following Easter, plus either Good Friday or the Tuesday following Easter)
- Memorial Day
- Juneteenth
- Independence Day
- Labor Day
- Indigenous Peoples Day
- Veterans Day / Martin Luther’s Birthday
- Thanksgiving Day (plus the Friday after Thanksgiving)
- Christmas Day (plus one additional day either before or after Christmas)
