Prompt
1. Remember an experience of God in your life. Get in touch with a time when you experienced a special presence of God in your life. A time when you felt most truly yourself with God, perhaps a time of deep clarity or knowing when you had some insight into your relationship with God. If you can’t recall a moment of special presence, remember a time when God seemed especially absent or distant.  
2. Recall some details. When, where, with whom?  
3. What was it like for you? What did it feel like? What was its "tone quality?" If you were composing a song about it, what would it sound like? If you were painting a picture of this feeling, what colors would you use, what would it look like?
4. Does the memory of this experience with the feeling come back to you sometimes? What circumstances have called it to mind?
5. When the details of the experience are stripped away, what does it seem God was saying to you? What is the underlying tone and grace of the experience—an invitation to trust? a sense of being loved? a challenge to compassion? a new or deepened sense of rootedness in God? etc.? How does that grace show itself in your life now? What evidence of it do you see in your relationships with others? In the choices you make?
6. Take some time in silence to rest in the grace of the experience. Just be there simply with God in whatever might be shown.
