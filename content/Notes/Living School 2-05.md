## Reflection Questions
We invite you to use the following prompts for your Deep Reflection journaling this week. Please take time to reflect on any three of the following questions. In addition, we have also provided a Spiritual Autobiography question for you to prioritize in your reflection time. 

What most surprised you or excited you about the monastic traditions?  

What was most troubling for you about the monastic traditions?   

What themes are emerging in the curriculum of your life so far this trimester? 

Imagine designing an order of life for yourself. What priorities would it involve? What would the overall schedule look like? How often might you want to revisit and potentially revise it? 

Spiritual Autobiography: As you look back on your own life and the last few months of the Essentials Course, what rhythm of life or order of life has begun to emerge for you?
