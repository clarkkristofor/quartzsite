[[12 fav problems, Ted Gioia]]

[[my calling]] [[Zion]] [[Jesus]]

1. How can I live and lead from a place of joy, wonder, and expectation of God's loving action and presence, instead of fear/ego that it's all up to me?
2. What's the right balance or integration of work and play?
3. How can we find Jesus “among all these cars?” In other words, 
    1. how can a congregation live into its gospel calling to be an alternative, spiritually-vital community serving the common good in a self-, career-, money-, control-, image/esteem- and security-focused culture?
    2. And how can a congregation shape everyday contemplatives in a world of distraction, chaos, and trauma?
4. How do congregational leaders stay faithful and hopeful in a season of shrinking institutional resources?
5. What's the difference between courage and wishful thinking? 
6. How can a congregation embody and teach the faith to a new generation raised with rote, irrelevant, or hypocritical religion?
7. How can a congregation do the above with the trust and support of the elder generation?
8. How can a congregation foster order-making among young people raised in chaos and order-surrendering among other-valuing elders?
9. What can it look like for a congregation to hunger and thirst for justice in a politically polarized culture? Or even simply defend democracy from its Christian enemies? Let alone make bold witness to the gospel’s liberating news for black, brown, poor, LGBTQI, women folk, and the earth’s life systems on the way to being liberating news for all?
10. How does Zion, a mostly middle class white congregation, build authentic bridges and mutuality with black, brown, and poor neighbors? And what truth and love can we speak and live about our neighborhood when white middle class people call it “bad” or the “ghetto”?
11. How do we navigate power and wealth differentials in our companionship with Kirangare Parish, Tanzania? While allowing ourselves to be transformed by then and the gifts we need to receive from them?
12. How can faith in Jesus Christ change people's lives?
