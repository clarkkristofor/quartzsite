# Vision for Zion's Sustainability 

- stay rooted in this neighborhood, this crossroads of 8th and Marquette
- get clearer about God's calling now--who we are and who we're not, what is ours to do and what is not 
- focus on improving the (spiritual) well-being of who is Zion now, not who we want Zion people to be in the future (get clearer on what (spiritual) well-being is and what we'll focus on)
- downsize the building (costs) not the staff
