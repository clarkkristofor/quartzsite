There's a (board/RPG/video) gaming convention in Davenport: Saturday, April 12, 2025 at the Scott Community College Urban Campus in downtown Davenport. I emailed the organizers, because I think it'd be cool to help a small local con be fun and successful. Here's what they said:

"Last year was our first EICC Gaming Conference. We had about 130 attendees and we hosted a Super Smash tournament, an open gaming room, and a few D&D/RPG events. Our main goals were to bring students and the community together through gaming and to raise money/food for our campus food shelters. After our success from our first year, we do plan to host another convention in April 2025. Our convention is open to the community and it is also family friendly.

"We have a form to sign up to run events: https://forms.office.com/r/FNex16Peit

"...we'd love to have you run an RPG event at the EICC Gaming Con! Last year, we ran three DnD events and a Dread RPG event. All of them were well attended. We set a limit of 4-8 players, depending on the preferences of the game master, and set blocks of four hours. We had dedicated classroom spaces for RPG events with access to a computer, projector, sound system, and large tables. We're able to adjust any of these to fit the needs of your game."
