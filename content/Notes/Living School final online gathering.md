### As a wounded healer, how do you share your wound without causing further wounding?
CAB: Ask God for direction in moment. What am I supposed to share?
Let go. Release.
The listening is more important than what I say.
Talk with God about X and only share Y.
God tells me things. I listen.

GR: Sit on my hands. Keep what's mine to myself.

JF: Work through internalized trauma. The deepest trauma is that is closes off access to experience of God's presence breath by breath. Sensitive to my tolerance level to pain.
You'll talk and I'll listen.
I'll ask a question, a real one.
You can't answer me without pausing to listen to yourself to answer me.
You're beginning to become present to yourself, and our alliance will grow.
Symptoms lessen, yes, but an inexpressible understanding that you matter.
And infinitely loved broken person.

BM: Temptation to vengeance against people who hurt us or to elevate own status as a victim. Affirm other person's value by the quality of our attentiveness. What we say is very often less important than the quality of our presence. The details can become distracting. We can steal the spotlight back on ourselves. "I have my own stories of... So it gives me some sense of what you're going through." That's often enough, to turn the spotlight back to them.

### How do we remain on our altered path of spiritual groundedness in the world? How have you found yourself encouraged to stay on this path? How do you stay grounded?

BM: What is it you feel like you're sucked into by the world? (Overwhelm.) Tell me what that overwhelm does to you? (Doubts. Fear. Pessimism. Go in head more than heart.) When you go in and feel these feelings, what do you feel isn't good about that? (It takes me out of being capable of listening, hearing, really being intentional with how I'm showing up with myself and others.) I had to make a decision to be present to a relative's problem. There's nothing wrong with feeling overwhelmed and with expressing we're feeling it. This is one of the elements of maturity we're all trying to grow into in this path. My world isn't the only world. I will set my problems aside. Or sometimes it's just not a good time.

JF: Remember moments when we were momentarily graced with presence of God. It wasn't that something more was giving. We momentarily experienced the presence that was always there. With that conviction in mind, what am I to do? I can't decide to have another mystical awakening. But I can choose to assume the stance that offers least resistance to it. Fidelity to daily rendezvous. Ask for grace not to break thread of underlying tie. Of course we're human and it will break many times but not from God's side. How can I nurture myself? Find just one person I know loves me. Trauma floods the field. Love and contemplation gives more field.

CAB: Superman. I think I'm starving. I need some scripture. Some wise saying to chew in lectio. My tank is empty. Childlike imagination to see I'm not alone. We're still part of CAC community, ancestors, cloud of witnesses. Reach into it.

BM: Sometimes in moments of overwhelm we reach for hope, but hope is complicated. Things might not be okay. Things might get worse. We don't want to give false hope. There's a dimension to world that every problem is solvable with the right technique. We feel obligated to to have something nice to say. But maybe its really an opportunity to grow beyond the need to have something nice to say. "I'm here with you, and I know God's love and presence are here with you." Instead of "Everything will be okay."

### How do I share what I received from Living School? How do we find words?

BM: Connect this with previous question. Seems woo woo. Do we have to say this is literally true? Everyone has a lot of fine print with word "God". How many of our ideas do we want to put on as required footnotes to that work? Or how much do we want to be pointing to something without requiring (others to adopt) our words or ideas?

CAB: Deep listening 90% before dropping in my 10%. My students are all over map. The first thing they want to know is "Do I care?" They've seen a lot of older people not follow through. I let them know I meditate. For practical tips, at end of [[Life After Doom]], appendix how to have conversations. [[Richard Rohr]] has a huge gift for this. They really don't care what I know until they know that I care.

JF: Being sensitive and attentive to the person I'm with. What do they want to hear or are they capable of hearing? With each person, in language that expresses what the relationship means to one another? How to turn information into transformation? Every time you say "I love you" it recreates the moment to experience and be changed by it.


### Certain people I find hard to see Christ's presence in. How do I get past the "Why?"? Why do they do the things they do? Why do I have to love them?

JF: At Last Supper, there was a traitor at the table. Things didn't get off to a good start. Borg, The Last Week of Jesus' Life: Pontius Pilate was coming from Rome, with soldiers etc. Jesus coming in from other side on a donkey. Which parade to we belong to? "Father, forgive them they know not what they do?" It's always been this way. How do we stand with respect to it? Just accept up front that we're already crazy. Jesus is God's response to us in our dilemma. How can I be present to this in the details of my life?

BM: A common question. You have good reasons for being angry and not just say everything's okay. What are those reasons? Bring them out. What are we hoping being angry with these people and not loving them will achieve? What will happen to us if we keep getting angry with others in proportion to how bad they are? Which could lead to some empathy for how they got the way they are, and invite us into another parade.

CAB: It's hard. Acknowledging it helps. We're trapped in a post-capitalist economy that is so cruel and unfair. But in God's economy , there's nothing small. I carry around my bible verses. I tell God, "I am so angry. I don't get this." Then sit still and know that I am God.

JF: Gandhi. MLK Jr. Mother Teresa did not drown in the suffering in Calcutta.

GR: [[The Tears of Things]], movement from anger to sadness to love. Can you allow the anger to go away and touch into the sadness? Grieving will lead to love. Believing they're a child of God. A child of God who's behaving very badly but a child of God.

### Passing down a first half of life container, while living into second half, or more contemplative initiation?
Not "they have to walk before they run" but "we don't want them to know running is possible."

BM: "I used to see it that way. I have serious misgivings about it now." If you mock the thing you're trying to move beyond, then people will react to your mocking tone. Taking what's best from scripture and tradition. The best will come to the top if we approach it with good teaching. When I read a bad student paper, I don't engage with argument, I ask, "What are you trying to say here? How can you try to prove it?"

## How do we know what is ours to do from the Holy Spirit vs from ourselves? Doing things lately that come to a dead end. Is this the Spirit?

JF: Discernment. Moments of our life when we become aware there are aspects of ourselves that we aren't aware of. We realize it behooves us to bring that into awareness. Discernment is to bring out into the open. But we meet resistance, because of shame or because of accountability.

CAB: At 64, a great breakthrough is knowing I am not God. That's freed up a lot of my discernment. "Please show me what to do next?" Brother Lawrence says, "Return to God throughout the day." "Ask God for help before you do something. If God helps, say thank you. If not, maybe next time." Tree dropped limb. There are things I have to let go of. Things change. "The Holy Spirit moves through us like sap through a tree." Teresa of Avila. No tree exists alone.

GR: Discernment ultimately happens in community.

BM: The desire to find what is mine is the important thing.
