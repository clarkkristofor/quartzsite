building relationships

When you showcase your work in places with others who haven't "made it," you're also assumed to have not "made it" yet... The context in which you share your content matters a lot. It presets our expectations and how we receive it.

Social media is the great digital subway. It attracts tons of attention, but it is a low-trust environment...

When good content is presented in the context of a **high-trust environment**, it tends to perform well.
