1. Is this buying me time or stealing it? Spending money to save time makes people happier. How will I use that time? 
2. Is this a story or just a thing? Experiences beat possessions. Doing beats having. Six months from now will I be glad I did this?
3. Does this bring me closer to other people? Solo joy plateaus. Shared joy compounds. Who benefits from this spending?
4. Can I make this a treat instead of a baseline? Frequency kills enjoyment. Scarcity restores it. Can I structure this as an occasional treat? Make it a treat not a habit.
5. Can I pay now and enjoy later? Anticipation is a form of happiness. Can I frontload the cost and backload the joy? 

Three or more, probably a good idea. 

No = 0, Undecided = 1, Yes = 2
6+, probably a good idea
4 or less, skip it
