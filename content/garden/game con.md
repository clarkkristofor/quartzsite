Short for "game convention." A day- or days-long gathering in a convention center all about playing games (board, card, rpg, and/or video games), like [Gamicon](http://www.gamicon.org/) or the [EICC Games Convention](https://eicc.edu/eicc-for/current-students/gaming-convention.aspx) or [GenCon](https://www.gencon.com/).
