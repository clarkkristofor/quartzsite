# The Christian Life

> [!summary]- Description
> Science is based on fact, not theory. Good science always has a purpose. In The Science of the Christian Life, Dr. Jack Hyles focuses on helping Christians to improve their Christian lives by teaching the science of living Bible truth in all aspects of life. One who had observed the principled life by which Jack Hyles lived once said that he had made a science of the Christian life. This book will take the reader into a spiritual laboratory where Bible formulas teach us how we, too, can live a disciplined Christian life. This book contains volumes I & II, written by Dr. Jack Hyles.
