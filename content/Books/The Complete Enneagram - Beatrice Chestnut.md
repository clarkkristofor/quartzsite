# The Complete Enneagram

> [!summary]- Description
> Discover the 27 paths to transformation in Beatrice Chestnut's definitive guide to the Enneagram Beatrice Chestnut’s The Complete Enneagram has become a modern classic for anyone seeking to understand themselves and others. In this renewed edition, Chestnut offers her signature depth and clarity as she explores all nine Enneagram types and their 27 subtypes—revealing how our core patterns shape behavior, relationships, and growth. Featuring a new introduction by the author, this edition invites readers into Chestnut’s latest thinking after more than a decade of teaching and global Enneagram work. She offers new context, fresh insight, and guidance for using the Enneagram as a tool for awareness and transformation in today’s world. Comprehensive, compassionate, and endlessly illuminating, The Complete Enneagram remains the definitive guide to unlocking the patterns that keep us stuck—and the paths that set us free.
