# Christianity for the Rest of Us

> [!summary]- Description
> Bucking the conventional wisdom that only conservative churches are growing, the surprising results of Bass's investigation reveals just the opposite: non-evangelical, progressive churches are flourishing, and they are doing so without resorting to mimicking the mega-church, evangelical style.
