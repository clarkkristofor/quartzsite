# Reading Judas: The Gospel of Judas and the Shaping of Christianity

> [!summary]- Description
> After briefly discussing the finding of the "Gospel of Judas," which was written around 150 C.E., this book states that this gospel opens a window onto the disputes among second-century Christians over the meaning of Judas's betrayal and Jesus's teachings.
