# A Sense of Urgency

> [!summary]- Description
> Argues that for true change to occur, organizations need to develop a sense of urgency and root our complacency. Key tactics recommended are: bringing the outside in, behaving with urgency everyday, finding opportunities in crises and dealing effectively with naysayers.
