# Lutheran Book of Worship

> [!summary]- Description
> Since 1978 the "LBW" has been the primary worship resource for the Evangelical Lutheran Church in America (ELCA). This single volume - affectionately known as "The Green Book" - contains worship liturgies as well as a complete hymnal. A complete family of LBW volumes provides a variety of formats, other liturgies, accompaniments, commentary, selections, and more.
