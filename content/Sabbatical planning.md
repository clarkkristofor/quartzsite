## Part 1: London, from SE1

### Getting around from the Airbnb

The Airbnb sits in one of the best-connected, most walkable parts of London.

|Nearby|Walk from Airbnb|Useful for|
|---|---|---|
|**Borough station**|~8 min|Northern line (Bank branch): Angel, King's Cross, Euston|
|**London Bridge station**|~10 min|Jubilee line (Westminster, Green Park, Baker Street, Waterloo), Northern line, Thameslink trains|
|Borough Market / Southwark Cathedral|~10 min|Food, South Bank walks|
|Tower Bridge → Tower of London|~20–25 min|Bank holiday Monday|
|Tate Modern / Globe / Millennium Bridge|~25 min along the river|Wednesday|

**Paying:**

- Tap a contactless card or phone at the gates on tubes, buses, and trains. Fares cap automatically each day.
- **Each person needs their own card or device.** You can't tap one card for several people.
- Children under 11 generally travel free with a paying adult. Confirm the current TfL rules for your child's age.

**Apps and checks:**

- Citymapper or Google Maps handle routing well.
- Check tfl.gov.uk/status-updates for weekend closures before Saturday and Sunday.

**Heat:** The tube gets hot. On warm days, walk or take buses for short hops.

### Fri May 28 — Your arrival

- Arrive at Brussels Midi mid-morning on the Nightjet, then take the Eurostar to London (about 2 hours). You'd be in London by early afternoon.
- The Airbnb starts May 29, so book a separate place for tonight. Alternatively, spend the night in Brussels and take a morning Eurostar on Saturday, as long as it arrives in time to meet the family at Heathrow.
- If you're in London, rest. Consider Evensong at Westminster Abbey or St Paul's.

### Sat May 29 — Family lands at 11:30am

- **Getting there:** Meet them at Heathrow.
    - **Transit (about 75 min):** Elizabeth line to **Farringdon**, change to a Thameslink train to **London Bridge**, then walk 10 minutes to the Airbnb. Both trains are step-free and handle luggage well.
    - **Door-to-door fallback:** Uber or a black cab (roughly £60–100) if everyone is wiped out.
- Check in mid-afternoon and rest.
- **Optional early-evening stroll:**
    - Take the Jubilee line from London Bridge to **Green Park** (3 stops).
    - Walk through Green Park to **Buckingham Palace**, then along St James's Park to **Westminster** for Big Ben.
    - Jubilee line home from Westminster.
- Easy dinner, early bed.

### Sun May 30 — Platform 9¾, Angel brunch, Arsenal v Brighton

- **Morning:** Sleep in, since this is the family's jet-lag recovery day. Then take the Northern line from Borough or London Bridge to **King's Cross** (about 12 min) for Platform 9¾. Go early, because the photo queue grows.
- **Brunch:** Ride one stop to **Angel** and have brunch at **Brother Marcus** on Camden Passage. Afterward, browse Camden Passage's antiques and curios. The Regent's Canal is a block away if you want a stroll.
- **To the Emirates:** Walk up Upper Street, about 25–30 minutes and pleasant. Allow extra time for crowds.
- **After the match:** The stations nearest the stadium get crushed. Walk back to **Angel** and take the Northern line straight home to London Bridge or Borough.
- Confirm kickoff time and tickets closer to the date.

### Mon May 31 — Tower of London and Leadenhall Market (bank holiday)

- **Morning:** Walk over **Tower Bridge** to the **Tower of London**, about 20–25 minutes on foot. Go early and book ahead, since it's a bank holiday.
- **After the Tower:** Walk about 10 minutes to **Leadenhall Market**, the Victorian covered market used as a Diagon Alley location in the Harry Potter films. Some shops may be shut on the bank holiday, but the architecture is the point.
- **Afternoon:** Nothing planned.

### Tue Jun 1 — British Museum, afternoon tea, West End

- **Getting to the museum:** Jubilee line to **Waterloo**, change to the Northern line (Charing Cross branch) to **Tottenham Court Road**, then walk 5 minutes. About 25 minutes total.
- **Morning:** Pick two or three galleries at the British Museum.
- **Stationery stop:** Present & Correct is a tiny, beautiful stationery shop at 12 Bury Place, around the corner from the museum.
- **Midday:** Head home to rest, or linger nearby.
- **Afternoon tea:** Book a late sitting at **The Ritz** or **sketch** so it doubles as an early dinner. Both are a splurge. The Ritz is by Green Park station, which is on the Jubilee line straight from London Bridge.
- **Evening:** West End show. Most theatres are a 10–15 minute walk from Green Park.
- **Getting home:** Jubilee line from Green Park direct to London Bridge. Or, on a nice night, walk to Embankment and cross the river.

### Wed Jun 2 — South Bank day and Twinings (all on foot)

- Walk 10 minutes to **Borough Market** for lunch. **Flat Iron Square** next door is the less crowded alternative.
- Pass Southwark Cathedral and follow the river west to the **Millennium Bridge** and **Tate Modern**.
- **Shakespeare's Globe:** take a daytime tour, or come back for an evening performance.
- **Twinings:** Keep walking west along the river, then cross **Waterloo Bridge** to the Twinings shop at 216 Strand, which has been there since 1706. Check its closing time, and go before it shuts.
- If you have more energy, the Southbank Centre, National Theatre, and BFI are all along the same stretch of river.
- Walk or Jubilee line home.

### Thu Jun 3 — Check out, then split up

- **Morning:** Check out. Use any free time for a last neighborhood walk or a leftover item from the list.
- **You and your son:** Take the train from **Euston** to **Birmingham International**, about 1 hr 10 min direct. The NEC, where Games Expo is held, is connected to the station by walkway. Stay nearby tonight so you're there for the 9am opening on Friday.
    - To get to Euston: Northern line from Borough or London Bridge, about 10 minutes, no change.
- **Wife and daughter:** Undecided (see Part 2).
