# session notes
## 3. well of shadow
Marwen found Donny the ravages elf and his three-tailed fox demon buddy in the lighthouse. Despite theological disputes about demon society in the Hells and uses of demons in this world, they managed to:
- banish the demon possessing the thug
- relight - with Terry's help - the western lighthouse
- kill the other three March Lord thugs, with a demon from Marwen's stone ([[#Donegal (Donny) the Fox|Donny]])
- share a delightful stew
- convince Terry to give them a whole rat filled with the light of heaven (living things only)
- find the source of the shadow mists, with aid from Marwen's ancestor spirit
- get rocked by a crab demon and feign death ([[#Marwen Maxan the Zealot|Marwen]])
- flee in terror (Donny)
- get dragged by the crab demon (Marwen) toward a shadow-mist spewing well beside a ruin of a house
- bind that crab demon to his rock (Marwen), only to awaken a huge blobby frog demon in the well
- return with renewed courage, awaken the oppressed energies of the Wild, and throw the now-Wild-engorged-light-bearing rat onto the huge frog demon (Donny, double 6s)
- in trying to bind that demon to his rock, encounter the shadow itself (Marwen), which played (honestly or not) on his ideological sympathies and asked him to thrown his rock into the well, while the rock-bound crab demon promised more demons for his rock if he freed it. Marwen did neither, but did unbind all the demons into the well and kept his rock, thank you very much.
- hobble merrily back to the western lighthouse for some well-earned downtime, and maybe more stew
## 2. trying to relight their lighthouse
Escaping the March Lord ambush, Marwen teleported himself, incapacitated Gashnag, and the unconscious thug bearing the light of heaven into the western lighthouse. But the light went out, in the thug and in the lighthouse.
