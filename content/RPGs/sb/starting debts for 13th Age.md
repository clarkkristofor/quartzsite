## Classes
**Barbarian**: You incurred the wrath of a powerful person; someone helped smooth things over. You owe them a Debt.
**Bard**: Someone hired you for a job and you fucked it up. Tell them why another obligation got in the way. You owe them a Debt.
**Cleric**: You intercede with the divine on someone's behalf. Ask them why they need your help; tell the GM what you’ve provided and ask how many Debts (1-3) you’re owed.
**Fighter**: Someone relies on you for training or knowledge. Ask them why they need your help; tell the GM what you’ve provided and ask how many Debts (1-3) you’re owed.
**Paladin**: You secretly helped someone get justice for a wrong done upon them. They owe you a Debt. Tell them why you helped.
**Ranger**: Someone has enlisted you to protect them from something dangerous. They owe you a Debt.
**Rogue**: Someone relies on you for their fix. Ask them what you provide that keeps them sane. They owe you a Debt.
**Sorcerer**: Someone helps you unwind and keeps you sane, despite the chaos of your magics. You owe them a Debt.
**Wizard**: Someone is your go-to when you get into trouble, providing information or muscle to get things done. You owe them 2 Debts.
**Chaos Mage**: see Sorcerer.
**Commander**: see Fighter.
**Druid**:
**The Occultist**: Someone interfered with your destiny. They owe you a Debt. Tell them if you’ve forgiven them—they owe you another Debt if you still hold a grudge for their actions.

## Kin
**Human**: Someone puts up with your questions about their kin. You owe them a Debt.
**Dwarf**: You are keeping something hidden on behalf of someone else from a powerful member of an icon's organization. Ask them why. They owe you a Debt.
**Half-Orc:** You offered someone work when no one else would give them the time of day. Ask them if it worked out in your favor. They owe you a Debt either way.
**High Elf**: Someone regularly relies on you for your learning, artistry, or magical prowess. They owe you 2 Debts.
**Silver Elf**: You partner with someone on your schemes, both of you profiting in equal measure. You owe each other 2 Debts.
**Wood Elf**: Someone lives in your territory, benefiting from your kin's protection. They owe you a Debt.
**Gnome**: You’re working on something big for someone, and it’s nearly ready. They owe you a Debt.
**Halfling**: Someone keeps pulling your ass out of the fire when you forget you’re small. You owe them a Debt.
**Dragonic**: 
**Holy One**: Someone helps decipher your calling or purpose with unique insights. You owe them 2 Debts.
**Forgeborn**: Someone helps decipher your calling or purpose beyond your creators intention with unique insights. You owe them 2 Debts.
**Tiefling**: Someone, or someone’s progenitor, was involved in your demon-touch. Ask them whether they regard it as a blessing or a curse. If a blessing, they owe you a Debt; a curse, you owe them a Debt.

You intervened on behalf of someone who had crossed a powerful member of your kin, saving their life. They owe you a Debt.
Someone stole something from you on behalf of the Ordo Draconis. Ask them if they knew who they were working for at the time. Either way, they owe you a Debt.
Someone is looking after a broodmate—someone you have thus far been unwilling to approach. Tell them what you fear may happen; you owe them a Debt.

## Icons
**Archmage**: You had a dark vision about someone, but gave bad guidance. You owe them a Debt.
**Crusader**: Your service forced you to punish or kill someone’s ally or friend on behalf of your masters. You owe them a Debt.
**Diabolist**: Someone is actively watching over one of your loved ones. Ask them why they agreed to keep them safe. You owe them 2 Debts.
**Dwarf King**: Someone recently sold you out to one of your enemies. You avoided the worst of the attacks, but your betrayer owes you a Debt.
**Elf Queen**: Someone disrupted a rare ritual of the Queen for personal gain, besmirching your reputation at court. They owe you a Debt.
**Emperor**: You are helping someone keep a dangerous secret from powerful members of their icon's organization. They owe you a Debt.
**Great Gold Wyrm**: You’re sworn to protect someone from a dark power. Your charge owes you a Debt.
**High Druid**: You entrusted someone with an important and dangerous task. Ask them if they succeeded or failed. If they succeeded, you owe them a Debt. If they failed, they owe you a Debt.
**Lich King**: You’re leveraging dirt you have on someone to get their help dismantling a necromantic scheme that targets innocent mortals. You owe them a Debt.
**Priestess**: You are trying to save someone from damnation and keep suffering for it. Tell them why you care when no one else does. They owe you a Debt.
**Prince of Shadows**: Someone gives you info about an icon's organization that you don’t understand. You owe them a Debt.
**The Three**: Someone intervened on your behalf when you crossed a powerful figure from another icon's organization. You owe them a Debt.

Someone befriended you long before you discovered magic and the adventuring life...and purposefully hid it from you when it mattered. They owe you a Debt.
