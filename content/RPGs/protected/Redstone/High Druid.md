"The High Druid calls up the primeval power of nature and shares it with those who know the ancient ways. Druids make more use of her magic than anyone else, but plenty of regular people across the Empire follow the ancient ways with rites and prayers of nature and fertility. Spirits of the wild, intelligent trees, talking animals, and brutal elementals all incline toward her, and she shares her power with them. Even ordinary plants and animals grow strong and healthy under her influence, and wolves howl to both her and the moon" (HH249).
