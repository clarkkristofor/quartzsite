- controlled by House Abulyrd (Crusader), by decree of the Emperor

### Camp Booming Chasm
- orc smuggling camp run by Raddick, a veteran smuggler loyal to the Orc Lord
- Mas Merri, freed from the Drek prison in Drakkenhall, stays here

### Eyrie of Zilharr the Radiant
- Zilharr is a great silver dragon loyal to the Great Gold Wyrm
- home to the Ardent of Zilharr the Radiant
    - Fyrl is Zilharr’s Sanganac
    - Brother Anthon, freed from the Drek prison in Drakkenhall, stays here
