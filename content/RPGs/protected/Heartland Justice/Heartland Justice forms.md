## **3/28/24: Heartland Justice!**

### AEGIS recognition ceremony
*purpose*: celebrating your victory, awarding you “shields”—giving you higher status within AEGIS (earlier than usual), but even more, assuring public everything is OK, reinforcing narrative that X acted alone
- Mayor, [[ Storm Lightning]] (Blink/Miles Murphy/Sean’s character’s mom), and **AEGIS director, Russell Sun**, all speak
- AEGIS assigns Blink as new team leader, names your team “Heartland Justice”
- Agent Rayburn shares X’s identity—Leon Skum—scion of tech billionaire and SkumTech founder, Julius Skum, who is also a major donor to William Patton’s mayoral campaign; also SkumTech and PDG are about to finalize a smart home development deal that will displace Abed’s mosque
- AEGIS director hints at need for new generation of heroes to step up, as some major new operation is coming
### doxxed
team goes to a Lightning family safehouse to celebrate; Abed steals a Lightning motorcycle and rides to Wisconsin, where he gets a call from his mom—X stans doxxed Abed on Twitter, Abed’s mom forced to flee apartment, AEGIS Family Protection Program takes action, Abed does not go to her
### the Supe
team goes to the Supe, the supers club in Boys Town
- Supers with shields get in free, normies pay huge cover
- Chicago’s main team of supers are the [[Exemplars]]—every era has a team of Exemplars, currently the they are Nighthawk, Sledge, Dr. Carmichael, Mist, and Planetar
- Sledge is the super everyone wants to be near, life of the party, your status marked by how close your table is to his
- TV at the bar: NASA’s Planetary Defense tracking a distant asteroid headed for earth, NASA in talks with AEGIS to deal with it
- team embarrasses [[Halftime]]—blonde, blue eyed, white super, grandson of supervillain [[Hourglass]]who blinded Blink’s grandpa, [[Bolt Lightning]]; Halftime’s father rejected Hourglass and served AEGIS for years
### getting into CU
team somewhat drunk goes to AEGIS HQ to interrogate X themselves at like 2am
- X in AEGIS Containment Unit awaiting trial
- head guard, **Agent Dan Jansen**, won’t let them in; says Planetar oversees the CU, talk to him; team tries other options
    - Abed calls **Agent Rayburn,** hands phone to Diego—ends up pissing off Agent Rayburn, by asking him for too big of a favor
    - Blink plays Lightning family legacy card to get in to see X; Jansen lets them through
### interrogating X
team talks to X, who is a smug asshole
- The Glint almost nukes the place with 4 conditions, calms himself with prayer he learned from Imam
- Blink pierced his mask, learning Leon wanted to capture not kill the Mayor; he was trying to prove to his father supervillaining is a better path to power than politics; he “hasn’t played all his cards,” threatened more than just doxxing
- left X considering giving them dirt on his father, Julius Skum; X said he’d be in touch via lawyerly backchannels
