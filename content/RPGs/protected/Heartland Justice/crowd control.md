# crowd control
*Masks: Heartland Justice*, 2025-03-27

## opening panels
- Storm skips across Lake Michigan like a stone
- out of control, she skids to a stop on a beach in St. Joseph, Michigan
- a little boy approaches her; she's unconscious
## crowd converging
- Miles slowed down the marchers from Buckingham Fountain by placing barriers at super-speed
- Miles took a barrier from the stationary protest cordon, and about a dozen protesters got through
- Diego intimidated them back behind the cordon
- Lizzy scouted ahead and got caught up in the crowd and took a hit
- Miles came for her but got bogged down in the crowd
- Clarke came to her rescue
- Lizzy got free, and when embracing Diego,  saw the Miles in the crowd and the Miles with them by the entrance to AEGIS HQ
- but getting some hits in on a super revved up the crowd
## candid camera
- the heroes tried to negotiate but they soon realized the crowd was too hyped up
- Lizzy fired a camera arrow into a prime spot, putting protestors up on the big screen, and identifying when they or their loved ones were saved by
- this livestream successfully shamed many protesters and took wind out of their sails
## more exoskeletons
- a handful of exoskeleton thugs moved forward through the crowd; it parted to let them through
- they reached the line held by the heroes
- the heroes refused to initiate a fight but took many hits, and risked themselves to protect each other and protestors
- all of this was livestreamed too
- the heroes prevailed
- pressed by a hero, one thug admitted Julius Skum, SkumTech CEO and father of Leon/X, had given them the exoskeletons
## will the real Miles please stand up?
- the heroes feared there was an imposter Miles
- Lizzy brokered peace between the group and the two Miles's, using a secret Miles had told her to prove there was not an imposter
- there was a time traveler instead
## but words will never hurt me
- the team supported and comforted each other
- but when Storm, Miles's mom, came down, she was shocked to see two Miles's but then pulled them both in for a hug
- but Miles<sub>0</sub> (future Miles, Sean's Miles) pushed her away in anger and said, "I don't need you anymore."
- Storm was crushed
## closing panels
- back on the beach in St Joseph, Michigan
- Storm opens her eyes
- they are clear: her own normal eyes
