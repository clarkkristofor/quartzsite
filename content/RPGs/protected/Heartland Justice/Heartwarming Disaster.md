## **2024-05-09: Heartwarming Disaster**
### Lecture with Robbery footage
 - [[Storm Lightning]] and hungover Miles and Abed in Lightning safehouse
 - Storm shows security camera footage of several robberies of nearby businesses and a bank across the street
 - 7 Apple Vision Pro wearers, 5 in custody, no known ties to each other, claim no memory of events, no criminal records
 - Storm lectures Miles about family legacy
 - Abed comes to Miles's defense
### Mandatory Leadership Training
- Agent Rayburn, Abed, and Diego - all BIPOC - in a small lecture hall in AEGIS HQ
- “Most supers think it’s all about blowing things up and running fast. It’s not. It’s about protecting regular people from the powerful who want to use and abuse them. Now I know you know that, but you gotta be smart. You make yourselves a target, you put everyone around you in danger.“ (Target of AEGIS, not villains.)
- Rayburn brings in [[Void]] (the Outsider, Dan) to train them
### Void's AEGIS system training
with shields, you have access to the AEGIS computer system—makes you put your hands in science goo, but there's an app and a web log in too—in the system you can:
- *view documents/files* (rosters and files on supers and villain and agents, investigation logs, inventory lists, deployment maps, operation briefs, R&D reports, CU roster—current and historical)
- *make requisitions requests* (purchase requisitions, investigative assets requisitions, prisoner interview requisitions, etc)
- *AEGIS org charts*—force vs cloaks, super prosecution, research, HR, PR
### Solving Crimes
- team plus detective in evidence storage at local CPD predict, Apple Vision Pro on table
- Diego asserts authority, cop gets in his face and pushes him against wall, as captain comes in
- Void tries to read cop's mind but is overwhelmed by his childhood trauma, gets angry about that trauma, and later tries to burn him - Blink defends the cop but gets burnt
- team follows Vision Pro location history to a park, reviews security cam at 7-11 and sees all 7 thieves at park throwing stolen goods into the back of a van, 2 get in van, 5 remain
### Vision Pros
- Clarke bum-rushes a baby snatcher wearing Vision Pros, with the Glint's help, but Clarke loses his shield
- with el Luchador's help, Blink and Void protect the baby and mother but
	- Blink lost control and severed the snatcher's arm
	- Void walked into traffic and got hit by a car, then lost control and fireballed a Starbucks
- Clarke calls in an AEGIS Rapid Response Team and then shakes the Vision Pros off the snatcher, who is bleeding out, horrified, in terrible pain, and now suddenly bewildered; Blink rushes him to the hospital
- two other Vision Pro dudes are livestreaming the mayhem but the Glint creates a faraday cage out of reality and el Luchador removes one's Vision Pro; Void, the other's; and they too are bewildered
- *the Glint's Moment of Truth* - he revises reality to put out the Starbucks fire, repair the car that hit Void, eliminate any record of the livestream, and recover Clarke's shield
### *fin*
- the team interrogates the two livestreamers (one young professional, one stoner) at Starbucks
- they have no memory since the previous evening, before the robberies
- Void probes the mind of the stoner
- team members comfort each other, removing conditions, but Void goes to the park and mind-controls a bunch of humans to do the Thriller dance
