## Dual kinship
**Names:** Uses the conventions of both kins.

## Troll
**Names:** Blackholm, Greyfell, Harrowtor, Mirebeck, Nettlecombe, Stagscar. *A landmark near their home ground.*

## Hearthborn
**Names:** Hob, Little Bede, Nan Hollis, Sisi Mahrang, Tam Cooper, Wynn of Dunhold. *Childlike echoes of their family.*

## Forged
**Names:** Breach, Ewer, Hauler Twelve, Nine, Seamfinder, Third Bellows. *Typically, their function or designation. Wildbuilt name themselves or are named for the materials they're built from*: Antler-and-Loam, Bramble, Kindly, Mossback, Riverstone, Wren.
