# Factions — GM Instructions

Factions aren't pre-authored. They emerge from culture stem rolls during play, then get fleshed out with a few fields as they matter.

## Trigger

Two or more kin share a Realm, stem, or specific roll result → that's a faction. The shared line _is_ its defining trait. No invention needed.

## Core Identity (from the roll)

1. **Who leads it** — the role or institution already rolled.
2. **What do they want** — the stem line already rolled.
3. **Ties** — rival or ally, drawn from the same axis:
    - Branching: the opposing posture.
    - Sequential: an earlier or later stage.
    - Lattice: a different column value (different god, sect, etc.).

Ties fall out of table structure. Don't invent them separately.

## Standing (fill in through play, not rolled)

- **Tier** — power relative to other factions. Starts low; earned through play, not assigned upfront.
- **Hold** — strong or weak grip on their turf. Shifts with events.
- **Turf** — always somewhere in and around Lost Plenty, within the Over Reach. Don't invent geography outside it; place the faction on the existing map.

## Face

- **Anchor NPC** — the kin who founded or now embodies the faction. Starts as a PC or named rolled figure, not an invented stranger.

Fill Standing and Face only once a faction becomes relevant at the table — don't front-load them.

# Factions — GM Instructions

Factions aren't pre-authored. They emerge from culture stem rolls during play.

## Trigger

Two or more kin share a Realm, stem, or specific roll result → that's a faction. The shared line _is_ its defining trait. No invention needed.

## Naming It

When a faction needs fleshing out, answer three questions from what's already on the sheet:

1. **Who leads it** — the role or institution already rolled.
2. **What do they want** — the stem line already rolled.
3. **Who's their rival or ally** — another faction on the _same axis_:
    - Branching: the opposing posture.
    - Sequential: an earlier or later stage.
    - Lattice: a different column value (different god, sect, etc.).

Relationships fall out of the table structure. Don't invent them separately.


**Section 1 — Named institutions/organizations (already function as factions)**

- Dead: council undying; second death squads; ghost courts (the dead's reckoning)
- Heavens: the six sects — ascetics, ecstatics, folk-healers, inquest, orthodox, utopians (each attached to a god)
- Hells: binders and their bloodlines (implied lineage/guild)

**Section 2 — Named roles/practitioner groups (occupational, not yet organizations — faction seeds)**

- Deeps (curious): tidecatchers, mycologists, somnologists, delver monks, tenebrologers, custos dementiae
- Deeps (hungry): wyrm whisperers, blind priests, vein speakers, voidmongers, inundated, raving oracles
- Deeps (devourer entities — not factions, but named horrors that could anchor cults around them): Drowned Prophets, Knell, Thirst, Vastness, Worldcoil, Zygote
- Heavens (gods, as rallying points sects orbit): herald, judge, maw, mother, trickster, weaver
