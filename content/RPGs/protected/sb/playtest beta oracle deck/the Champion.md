## *description*
Marwen seeks to recruit to his cult Lost Plenty's archery champion. Donny—whose kin dominate demons for sport—seeks renown in the Over Reach, that he might return to rule his homeland. You all, of course, seek victory over the shadow. And all the Over Reach knows what they paid for the Coastal Cities glory.
## *divination*
The Champion represents victory through strength and skill. It promises public acclamation and a rise in fortunes: prizes won, favor curried, adoration earned. It may call for more training to prepare for a future contest or challenge or simply for perseverance because current struggles will give way to ease and glory. In some cases, it may forebode a loser's revenge or inflamed jealousies. In rarer cases, it speaks of a greater victory, which appears at first like defeat, arriving after strength is spent and skill fails and glories fade, whose prize is wisdom and inner strength.
