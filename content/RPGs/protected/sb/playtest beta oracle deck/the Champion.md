## *description*
Marwen seeks to recruit to his cult Lost Plenty's archery champion. Donny—whose kin dominate demons for sport—seeks renown in the Over Reach, that he might return to rule his homeland. You all, of course, seek victory over the shadow.
## *divination*
The Champion represents victory through strength and skill. It promises public acclamation and a rise in fortunes. In some cases, it may forebode a loser's revenge or inflamed jealousies. In rarer cases, it speaks of a greater victory. It appears at first like defeat, arriving after strength is spent and skill fails and glories fade, but its great prize is wisdom and inner strength.
