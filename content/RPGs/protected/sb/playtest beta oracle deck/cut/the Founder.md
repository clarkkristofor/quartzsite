## *other names*
- the Boot
- the Binder's Ghost
## *themes*
- inherited purpose
- captivity vs. destruction
- ancestral demand
- complicity

## *symbol*
an ancestor's shade standing between a chained demon and a waiting pyre, one hand toward each
## *description*
Marwen's Power calls itself the true founder of his clan, and it wants every demon ground beneath the world's boot—destroyed, not kept. Marwen binds them instead, chains them to a rock rather than to ash. The Founder has not forgiven him for this. It may never.
## *divination*
The Founder signifies an ancestor's demand at odds with your own judgment. What was founded expects to be finished on its own terms, and inheriting a legacy does not mean inheriting agreement with it. This card may appear when you choose to preserve what your forebears would destroy, or destroy what they meant to preserve—either way, marking you as unfaithful to the founding purpose even as you claim its power. Ask what you are keeping alive, and why, and whether the dead will let you.
