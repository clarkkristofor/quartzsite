## *other names*
- the Spilling
- the Whispering Mouth
## *themes*
- manipulation
- denial
- doubt
- evil bound vs. loosed
## *symbol*
an old well beside a ruined house, shadow-mist spilling over its lip like water running backward, half-formed faces in the fog
## *description*
Beside a house long since ruined, a well spills shadow instead of water. Something down there speaks kindly, reasonably, in a voice that sounds almost like your own convictions—and something else down there only wants to be let out. Whatever waits in the Well counts on you not being able to tell the difference.
## *divination*
The Well signifies a bargain that flatters you into believing it is a partnership. It appears when what's asked of you sounds reasonable—even righteous—and that is exactly the danger. The offer may be real. The kindness may be false. The Well does not tell you which, and neither will this reading. What it does promise is that something will be loosed, bound, or fed by your choice, and that the choice is closer than you think.
