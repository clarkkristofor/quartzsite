## *other names*
- the Unwavering
## *themes*
- silent devotion
- faith without sight
- inner certainty vs. outward doubt
## *symbol*
a blind, mute keeper standing watch at a lighthouse door, facing a light they'll never see but trust is there
## *description*
Toad cannot see the light he keeps. Terry cannot speak of what he believes. And still both stand their watches. Your own Zealots persist against the shadow, promising not to make followers of you.
## *divination*
The Faithful signifies conviction that needs no evidence. It may appear when your own certainty is being tested by someone else's doubt, or when you are tempted to press your beliefs onto someone unwilling to receive them. This card counsels a devotion that serves quietly, without demanding recognition or agreement—and warns that faith forced on another stops being faith at all. Sometimes the truest watch is kept by those who say the least about it.
