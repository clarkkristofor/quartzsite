## *other names*
- the Tear
- the Unmaking
## *themes*
- unraveling reality
- cleansing at a cost
- inherited duty
- forbidden power
- sacrifice
## *symbol*
a gatekeeper's hand pressed against a widening tear in the world, unnatural light spreading up her arm like a vein
## *description*
Gashng's mother stands gatekeeper over the tear that split when the Weft first bled into your world—unless she no longer can. The rift does not lie, exactly. It offers what it promises: sin unmade, unworthiness scoured clean. But nothing passes through untorn, and someone must always mind the gate.
## *divination*
The Rift signifies a true cure offered at an unbearable price, or a duty inherited before you were ready to carry it. Something in your life needs unmaking—an old wrong, a broken vow, a corrupted purpose—and the Rift offers to tear it out entirely. But tearing is not mending, and what the Rift removes, it removes for good. This card may also announce a passing of the torch: a guardian's post left open, a watch that now falls to you whether you asked for it or not.
