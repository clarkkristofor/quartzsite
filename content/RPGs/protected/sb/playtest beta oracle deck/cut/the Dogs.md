## *other names*
- the Outsiders
- the Chained Welcome
## *themes*
- conditional belonging
- being used
- loyalty unreturned
- outsider status
## *symbol*
hunting dogs chained at a threshold, guarding a warm house they're never let inside
## *description*
Lost Plenty houses you. Lost Plenty feeds you, when it can. But Lost Plenty does not, quite, let you in—not the way it lets in its own. You fight at the threshold of a warmth you guard but do not share. No conversions, the pact promised. No belonging either, though no one wrote that part down.
## *divination*
The Dogs signifies welcome with conditions attached, and loyalty given to someone who has not decided whether to return it. It may point to being valued for what you can do rather than who you are, or to a growing resentment at service unacknowledged. This card can also be an invitation: to demand better terms, to prove yourself indispensable rather than merely useful, or to finally ask whether the house is worth guarding at all if it never opens its door.
