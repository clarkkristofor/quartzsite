## *other names*
- the Green Flame
- the Untamed
## *themes*
- primal power
- healing through excess
- boundaries broken

## *symbol*
a small creature wreathed in uncontainable green-gold flame, cracking the stone beneath its feet
## *description*
Not all power answers to a Power. The Wild does not ask permission—it simply is, breaking stone and boundary alike wherever it is finally let loose. Donny's fox listens to something older than any pact; the light thrown at a well-demon burned brightest not because it was blessed but because it was, for one moment, unchained.
## *divination*
The Wildfire signifies a solution too large and too unruly to control cleanly. It may mean healing bought through excess—more force, more feeling, more life than the moment seems to call for—or a boundary that needed breaking long before it finally gave. This card warns that what saves you may also scorch what stands nearby; the Wild does not distinguish friend from obstacle. Still, when careful, contained answers have failed, the Wildfire is what's left.
