## *other names*
- the Unfathomable
- the Bled Vein
## *themes*
- hidden depths
- temptation
- isolation
- ancient hunger
- death
## *symbol*
far from shore, a small fishing boat is disturbed by something vast passing unseen beneath it
## *description*
The waters that separate Lost Plenty from the Coastal Cities are wide and deep indeed. All people of the Over Reach are people of the depths, in both the sea and the mines. For generations they dug deeper to feed the Cities' hunger. They look to the sea to feed their own. It costs them, even as it serves them.
## *divination*
You are at the mercy of forces too great or too old to be fully perceived and understood, let alone controlled. Proceed with the utmost respect and care. Make a costly offering, and you may receive abundant mercy. Alternatively, the Sea could point to abundance that lies further out than seems safe to travel or past where others will follow. It could warn of imminent death by drowning, thirst, or worse. Enraged and famished, something is finally surfacing to collect what it's owed.
