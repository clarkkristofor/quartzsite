## *description*
The March Lord is corrupt, as are his auditors. The Hells are the ultimate source of corruption. And that's what the shadow is too. 
## *divination*
Something good and true has been perverted. Maybe an advantage has become a disadvantage or a relationship has soured. In other cases, this card promises that, as invincible or overwhelming as an evil may seem, there remains a core that can be salvaged, cleansed, redeemed, put to good use. Corruption could also bring a hollow victory: you get what you want but it comes as harm not benefit.
