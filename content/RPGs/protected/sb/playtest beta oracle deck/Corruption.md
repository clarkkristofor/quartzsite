## *symbol*
a hand covered in pustules, fingers wreathed in fine rings, grasping gold coins so hard they drip with blood
## *description*
The March Lord is corrupt, as are his auditors. The Hells are the ultimate source of corruption. And that's what the shadow is too. 
## *divination*
Something good and true has been perverted. Maybe an advantage has become a disadvantage or a relationship has soured. In other cases, this card could be a call for mercy and creativity and a warning against violence or force. Because, as invincible or overwhelming as an evil may seem, there remains a core that can be salvaged, cleansed, redeemed, put to good use. But it will be risky, costly, requiring surrender, great patience, restraint, courage, suffering. Corruption could bring a hollow victory: you get what you want but it comes as harm not benefit. Or it could warn that pride cometh before the fall.
