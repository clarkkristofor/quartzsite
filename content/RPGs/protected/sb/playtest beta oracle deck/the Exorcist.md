## *symbol*
a circle of salts, scorched and scuffed in places, but intact, holding; within the circle, a glorious absence
## *description*
You all hunt demons, so, in a way, you are all exorcists. Physic trusts in his demonic bloody coin, and still he seeks in Lost Plenty the Exorcist, who knows his birth story. What might he lose to gain that story?
## *divination*
An exorcism doesn't leave you with something better. When the sulphur clears, it just leaves room. The Exorcist means a demon is cast out, the shadow suffers a setback, or the March Lord's profit turns to loss. And it also asks, what will you fill? Where will you advance? Who will gain instead? Sometimes, the Exorcist also announces a bargain unmade, a source of power eliminated, an orphaning.
