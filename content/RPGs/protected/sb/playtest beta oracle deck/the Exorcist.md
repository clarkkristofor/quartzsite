## *other names*
- the Truth-Sayer
- the Unwanted Cure
## *themes*
- possession banished 
- innocence restored
- secrets revealed
- the price of knowing and being known
## *symbol*
a circle of salts, scorched and scuffed in places, but intact, holding; within the circle, a glorious absence
## *description*
You all hunt demons, so, in a way, you are all exorcists. Physic trusts in his demonic bloody coin, and still he seeks in Lost Plenty the Exorcist, who knows his birth story. What might he lose to gain that story?
## *divination*
A demon is cast out. The shadow suffers a setback. The March Lord's profit turns to loss. At the same time, the Exorcist also announces a bargain unmade, a source of power eliminated, an orphaning. An exorcism doesn't leave you with something better. When the sulphur clears, it just leaves room.
