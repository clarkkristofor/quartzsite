## *description*
Your mother, sister, wife, children, cousin. You left them behind. They struggle and suffer without you. Lost Plenty is not your home. They are.
## *divination*
This card signifies an unavoidable reckoning with someone or something that was left behind. Unfinished business or an emotional entanglement interrupts and demands resolution. A loose end trips you up. It might be time for a thoroughgoing reappraisal of the past or of current commitments. Perhaps you can finally be free of something that has haunted or dogged you. But seizing this opportunity will be irrevocable: surrender, loss, death, or another ending. And not taking it will exact its own toll.
