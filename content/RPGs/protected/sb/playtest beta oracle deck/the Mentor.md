## *other names*
- the Old Hand
- the Unbeaten
## *themes*
- practical wisdom
- a lesson
- discipline
- enemies bound
## *symbol*
a length of knotted cord, weathered soft from use, coiled beside a tally of small notches cut into worn wood, more marks than anyone could count at a glance
## *description*
Someone taught Donny to summon a demon, bind it tight, and walk away whole. Sport, his kin call it. His mentor made him a winner. All of you learned from someone surer, more disciplined, and wiser than you. Somewhere, your enemies mentor apprentices in the shadow.
## *divination*
The Mentor means victory or the correction that prepares you to win tomorrow.
