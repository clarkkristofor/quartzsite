## *other names*
- Doom
## *themes*
- temptation
- wickedness
- inherited debt
- impotence
- unworthiness
## *symbol*
a sleeping infant swaddled in chains
## *description*
Gashnag trusts his tear in reality is the true way to cleanse sin and unworthiness. Orcs know sin, cursed as they are by the sins of ravenkin. Lost Plenty people know sin too. The whole Over Reach suffers for the sins of the Coastal Cities. Is the shadow a punishment for sin?
## *divination*
A reckoning has come. Sin cares not if the wrong was yours or another's; it merely announces inescapable punishment. Sometimes Sin warns of temptation: to vengeance, shallow absolution, or false judges and fraudulent saviors. It can also show forth a harder way: real amends, honestly made.
