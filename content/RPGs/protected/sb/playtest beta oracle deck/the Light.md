## *other names*
- the Lightbearer
- the Hearth
## *themes*
- aid
- clarity
- cleansing
- safety
- warmth
## *symbol*
a lighthouse atop a rocky cliff beaten by terrible waves, its light clearly visible, piercing dark, stormy clouds
## *description*
Of course, there is Toad and Terry and sanctuary in your alliance with the Lighthouse Keepers who bear heavenly light. But they are not the only ones. Not least, there is also help from Beyond, and the pact itself which is light for others in the face of the shadow.
## *divination*
Salvation is close at hand, maybe it rises even now like welcomed dawn. The way may yet be treacherous, but there is guidance or the promise of help or safe harbor ahead. To those sensing no urgent threat, the Light may come as a timely warning: seek shelter now! To the wicked or untrue, this card could mean disaster. The Heavens themselves oppose you.
