## *description*
Your Power Beyond favors you for a reason. That reason may not be about you and what you want. You made promises, of course, to the people of Lost Plenty and the Over Reach. Maybe your Power asks for something more, while offering more in return. Then again, perhaps the one who asks and offers is a pact-mate, not your Power. Or someone else entirely.
## *divination*
The Called signifies your dependence on a source Beyond and others' dependence on you. Often, it is an invitation to seek that source or recommit to that higher purpose. It may warn of the folly of forgetting that with great power comes great responsibility and with great responsibility comes great power.
