## *symbol*

## *description*
Your Power Beyond favors you for a reason. That reason may not be about you and what you want. Toad cannot see the light he keeps. Terry cannot speak of what he believes. And still both stand their solitary watches.
## *divination*
The Called signifies your dependence and your duty. Often, it comes as promise and warning. Keep faith with your Power Beyond. Seek it as a stream seeks the sea. Do your duty to Lost Plenty and the pact. Serve quietly without making demands. Otherwise, you may run dry. Maybe your Power asks for more from you, while offering more in return. Maybe the one who asks and offers is a pact-mate, not your Power. Or someone else entirely. Sometimes, the Called announces a new calling or a new Caller.
