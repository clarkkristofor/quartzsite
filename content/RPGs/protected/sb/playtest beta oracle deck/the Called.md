## *symbol*

## *description*
Your Power Beyond favors you for a reason. That reason may not be about you and what you want. Toad cannot see the light he keeps. Terry cannot speak of what he believes. And still both stand their solitary watches.
## *divination*
The Called signifies your dependence and your duty. Your Power asks for more from you, either offering more in return or threatening to withhold favor. Maybe the one who asks and offers is a pact-mate, not your Power.
