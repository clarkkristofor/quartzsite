- the Wounding Healer
## *themes*

## *symbol*
a two-chambered copper alembic above a flame, a stream of iron ore dust rising into its base; two spouts curve from its bulbs: from the lower, molten gold drips into a waiting coin-mold; from the higher, wheat grain falls into open, cupped hands
## *description*
Gashnag seeks a promised remedy from the Alchemist, but it's Physic's kin who pursue alchemical transcendence. The March Lord's exchange is Over Reach suffering for gold. You hunt for another way.
## *divination*
Nothing is made, only paid for. The Alchemist comes to collect, or, if the cost has already been paid, to deliver. It also promises reversal: what's in motion can be redirected, paid for in resentment, retribution. In other cases, the Alchemist points to the most mundane as the way beyond. Arrogance will blow up in your face.
