The one that started with [[Cursed Myth the Elf Queen is Dead!.pdf|the Elf Queen is dead!]] And an elven myth-quest went wrong. And from Valor, into elf heaven... the Last Bastion of Heavenly Light, beseiged by the Godkillers (devil dwarves) and other devils. Chiefly, Gûlrymsúl, mythic enemy of elves, who was imprisoned in myth and for ages made myth his weapon, then tried to break free.
# Oramir the Seer
*Speaker (Holy One Cleric)*
- My right eye was blinded when I was touched by the bright gods. Now that eye sees visions of the gods’ realm.
- Defended temple, Compelled confession
# Revan the Bonehook
*Dark Elf (but who’d know?) Necromancer*
- I lived after a beheading at the hands of the Halos. I have no head, only a haze-filled cowl with a pair of glowing gray eyes.
- Necropolis emissary, the Defilement, Drakkenhall
# Duncan Flametorn
*Human Defender (Swordmage)*
- I slayed the Pirate Captain Crookshanks. When I wrenched my sword from its lifeless corpse, the flame on my sword had turned blue.
- Sigil Mastery Academy, Gauntlet, Floating Plateau
# Stink
*Goblin ​Ranger*
- Somewhere in a forgotten sub-basement of the Emperox's Cathedral is a dusty, cobwebbed, all-but-ignored little shrine... to Stink.
- Escaped horde, Mad Potato God
- whose goblin followers included Burpsneeze, Shart, Grummy, Nosebleed (dead), Pusslick, Grimtoe, Munch, Soft-Skull, and Runt, leader of Havoc Brigade
# Thasomi Copperbrand
*Dwarf Abomination*
- I was touched by the Lich King, and I lived (sorta).
- Magic item historian, POW, Cemetery of Heroes
# Gravel
*Stonehearted Knight (forgeborn) Sorcerer*
- I'm the only Stonehearted Knight to attain the rank of officer.
# Malthael
*High elf Bladesinger*
