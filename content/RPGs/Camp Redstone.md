## 7. Demon Killer
[[Milford Steel]] killed the [[Cult of the Right Height demon]] and got a magical tattoo. [[Mouse Bones]] used his icon connection with the [[Great Gold Wyrm]] to cleanse the sacrificial circle. He also received a magic item.

[[Adrianne]], a very young tiefling girl from the Abyss, appeared almost as soon as the demon was killed. She said Milf's new tatttoo said either "demon friend" or "demon killer." He said, "I killed a demon." She said, "Good."

We led the survivors back to camp. [[Shivon]] slipped away. [[Bartleby Stumps]] went back to the Owls Head Tavern. Everyone else found a place to be. Holly, the honey tongued priest who surrendered to us, was lost. Mouse Bones gave him 2 gold. He's staying at Holes with us.

- Will people suspect all halflings, including [[Dockety Bumblesprout]], are demonic, after this incident?
- Will the wildbuilt make legs for all the former right-heighters?

LEVEL to 3rd.
## 6. Cult of the Right Height
We rescued the real Fred Boulder from the basement of [[Wolfgang Puck]]'s Fox & Hound.

Leader [[Bartleby Stumps]] and the 45 members (including 9 other fully mutilated) were enthralled to a demon. We interrupted the new moon ritual in Owl's Head Grove, saved [[Bartleby Stumps|Bart]] and the rest, and banished the demon. [[Shivon]] helped. A traveling evangelist named Frank Lloyd Height converted Bart originally. (Ended session at escalation 5. Demon and priests not yet defeated.)
