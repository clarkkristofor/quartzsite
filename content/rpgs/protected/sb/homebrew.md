General Advice
Give ‘em what they want
Let the players’ choices–starting with character creation–guide you. They’re telling you what they want. Give it to them. You know what you want. Give that to them too. Ask what they want more of.
Just ask
Ask your players lots of questions, especially details about people, places, and things their characters would know. You can stall for time with questions. Or ask for a minute to gather your thoughts. Most importantly, lines/veils, Jay Dragon’s recent post.
Play to see what happens
Trust the rules. “Play to see what happens” is the principle behind them. The dice and the rules will work for you, creating interesting choices and problems for your players.
Be obvious
What happens next? Say the obvious thing. Don’t be creative, says Graham Walsley. Don’t overthink it. 
Make factions and villains
Use the players’ Powers Beyond and Subtypes as inspiration for factions, patrons, and villains. Brainstorm these between sessions.
