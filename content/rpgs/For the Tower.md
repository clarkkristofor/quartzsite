A playtest with the designer at Gamicon Bromine. Inspired by RPGs like For the Queen, Dread, and The Quiet Year and media like Silo. It's played with tarot cards and a Jenga tower. Here's [an older version](https://aquavertigo.itch.io/for-the-tower). 

There are three playsets: wizard's tower, ship, and HQ. We chose ship and set our game on a generation starship, The Ark, the last to leave a dying Earth. I played the Founder. There was Custodian, Developer and Visitor. 

I would play again and buy.
