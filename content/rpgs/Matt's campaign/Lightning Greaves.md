# Lightning Greaves
- magic item previously owned by the [[Prince of Shadows MC]]
- now sought by [[Nolak]] and the [[Nolak|Rustlers]] for the Prince
- it was too powerful to be cursed when it passed through the [[Drakkenhall]] camp, where sorcerer [[Keef the innkeep]] was forced to try to curse it
- the Lightning Greaves were named for the great speed they bestow upon their wearer, but they give delusions to weak wearers
- the [[Prince of Shadows MC]] helped someone (who?) create them
- Ersa might know where the Greaves are and only Mishra in Old Town knows where Ersa is
