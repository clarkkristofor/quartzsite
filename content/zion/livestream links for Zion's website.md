[Tithely help page for finding livestream links](https://help.tithe.ly/hc/en-us/articles/7246971234071-How-to-Find-Your-Video-URL)

1. Go to https://www.youtube.com/@zionlutheranchurch1854
2. On the worship video you want to add as a New Live Stream, click the three vertical dots in the lower left of the video thumbnail. Then in the pop up menu, select Share. Then click the blue Copy button. You'll get something like this: `https://www.youtube.com/live/Vk0XwrWiqEY?si=NFgSdCgShDwelHVk`
3. Paste this in the Tithely Live Stream URL bar. But you're not done.
4. Replace `live/` with `watch?v=` so it becomes this: `https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Vk0XwrWiqEY?si=NFgSdCgShDwelHVk`
5. That's it for the link.
6. Fill out the rest of the New Live Stream form. 
	- Service Title in this format "Worship: July 7, 2024" (without quotes)
	- Choose preacher from Speaker list
	- Choose Simulated Off, Countdown On, Chat Off
7. Put these links in the Notes window
	 [Bulletin, including songs and scripture](https://ziondavenport.org/bulletin)
	 [Make an offering today](https://ziondavenport.org/pages/give)

8. Then in the upper right, click the black Create Live Stream button
