Hi I'm Pastor Clark. It's my honor to welcome you to Zion Lutheran Church. My roots are in Pennsylvania. I grew up in Hershey, the Sweetest Place in Earth. But my family and I also have roots here in Davenport. My wife Sara is a Pastor at St Paul Lutheran Church in Davenport. We've lived here for 15 years. Our kids are Iowan. Susannah is in show choir at Central. She just started driving! How did that happen!? And Amos is in the creative arts academy at Sudlow. It's been a beautiful and blessed time rising strong from the pandemic, growing our food pantry ministry, renewing our 25 year companionship with Kirangare Lutheran Parish in Tanzania, and listening deeply to God, our neighbors, and each other.
