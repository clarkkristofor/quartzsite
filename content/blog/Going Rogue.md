C, H, and I played [Going Rogue](https://jumpgategames.itch.io/going-rogue), a GM less game inspired by Star Wars Rogue One and Andor. I Kick-started its new edition. 

It was a bit deep in the writers' room for C and H, but it hit a sweet spot for me, since everyone is essentially playing and GMing at the same time. They said they might get more used to it if we played again, which I'd love to do.
