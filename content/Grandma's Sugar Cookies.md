## GRANDMA'S SUGAR COOKIES
2 Cups Granulated Sugar
2 Whole Eggs
1 Cup (½ lb) Butter or Margarine
1 Cup Buttermilk
2 Teaspoons Baking Powder
2 Teaspoons Baking Soda
5 to 5 ½ Cups Flour

Cream margarine and sugar together until smooth. Add eggs. Mix well. Sift dry ingredients together to thoroughly blend the baking soda and baking powder with the flour. (I use one cup milk plus 1 teaspoon vinegar instead of buttermilk - Mix together and let set for 5 minutes). Add the dry ingredients and the milk alternately until thoroughly blended. Drop by teaspoons on to a greased cookie sheet. Bake in a preheated 350 F to 400 F oven. (375 F works well in my oven). Remove from cookie sheet while warm.
Cool on a rack, paper towels, etc. (I use an old table cloth)

## BUTTER CREAM FROSTING
1 LB 10X Sugar
½ Cup Butter or Margarine, softened
1 Teaspoon Vanilla
3 to 4 Tablespoons Milk

Whip the margarine until softened (may want to use the microwave for about 10 seconds)
Add about 1/4 cup sugar, vanilla and milk. Mix until smooth. Add remaining sugar. Beat until creamy. Frost cooled cookies. Sprinkle with Jimmies, Colored Sugar or decorate as desired. Let frosting set for about ½ hour prior to stacking. Store in an air tight container. Use wax paper in between layers.
