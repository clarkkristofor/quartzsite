This morning, after dropping the kids at school, I went to the [[hospital]] to visit [[Carol De Bruyn]]. I was expecting to find her just the same as Sunday. Waiting but okay. Waiting for doctors to solve the puzzle of her second heart attack, or perhaps with plans to send her to [[Iowa City]]. But I did not find her in her room, and the nurse told me she was in the [[ICU]]. I started to worry.

I found her in the ICU, looking somewhat haggard and choking up telling me about another heart attack and how Mike and Kristine? had to witness it.

She told me about a yearning to close the distance she felt between her and God, but at the same time having no appetite for prayer or bible study. Even her ZLCW Circle, which had disbanded and reformed because too many people died.

She said it's been since her son John died. She's not been angry, she said, like after her 45 year old husband died. Did she say what it was like?

She expressed fear that God would not allow her into heaven because her faith was too weak. She mentioned comparing her faith to [[Cary Scott]] and [[Anita Hagen]]'s. And their feeling of closeness with God.

What I tried to tell her was, don't be afraid, God loves you dearly and has drawn you close. Don't be afraid, you have faith, a wonderful, tender, compassionate faith. Don't try to live someone else's faith, live your own. I read [[Psalm 13]]. She resonated with it.

It was mostly a lot of listening. Bearing witness. I came away fired up about *seeing* being the problem. Like that quote [[Mike Petrow]] keep's bringing up in the [[Living School]]:

[[Carl Jung]] writes, "Christianity must indeed begin again from the very beginning, if it is to meet its high educative task. So long as religion is only faith and outward form and not experienced in our own souls, nothing of any importance has happened. The person who does not know this from their own experience may be a most learned theologian, but they have no idea of religion and still less of education. Even theologians fail to see that it is not a matter of proving the existence of the light, but of blind people who do not even know that their eyes could see. It's high time. We realize that it is pointless to praise the light and preach it if nobody can see it. It's much more needful to teach people the art of seeing."

The art of seeing. Teach people the art of seeing.

The curriculum is your life. Trust yourself. God will show you.

This is what [[Hike Church]] is about for me. Or can be.

But to quote Jung above to Zion people or to lecture, explain, talk at length...that's not the way. Shut up and let them look.
- What are you bringing?
- What are you seeing?
- Look at the trees, the birds. They trust, God loves you more.
