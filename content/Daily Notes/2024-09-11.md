GI First Aid (GIFA) Kit
1. Pepto Bismol OTC
2. Imodium OTC
3. Travelers Diarrhea antibiotics {3 pills, prescription needed)
4. Electrolyte powder: LMNT or Liquid I.V. Hydration Multiplier recommended
You must pack this. Because we will each stay at separate host families, each traveler needs their own GIFA kit. Put it in your daily carry bag so wherever and wheneverwe are in Tanzania, you have it

Treating Travelers Diarrhea
Be prepared to experience GI discomfort. Extra water and ginger tea may be all you need to recover. If not, use your GI First Aid Kit.
