[[Living School]] [[called to fundraising question mark 1]] [[Living School Journal]]

Last night, as I was falling asleep, I was thinking of meeting Mark Snover and Andy Deaven again. I was thinking of what my part in that crack up was. If on the one hand, I was uncomfortable with smoking and with the disrespectful way they were talking about young women, I also was, on the other hand, less than loyal and less than courageous. I never fought for the relationship. 

I never spoke with them directly about these things. I never risked open conflict and showed up as myself. Instead, I talked behind their backs--to Andy about Mark, while we played basketball at that Methodist Church. 

Now it all may have played out in a very similar way. We may not have reconciled. Maybe they would've been as cruel as I--did I imagine it? I avoided eating in the cafeteria for a reason. That car ride home was painful for a reason. I didn't take personally something that was not even about me, did I? 

Well, this at least would be different now. I would know for sure, if I had spoke to them face-to-face, one-on-one. By moving my own self from covert to over, I'd've implicitly challenged them to do the same. And that's why I probably didn't do it. It hurt too much as it was. I chose nebulous. It was self-protection. Maybe it would've gone as badly as I feared but on the other hand, I'd've known for sure.

To be fair to all of us, we were children: 15, 16 years old. I didn't have the language... I didn't know how to have a conversation like that. I guess, even as an adult, do I? It's hard for adults, let alone adolescents. Hard for me.

Maybe the real problem was, **I had no trusted adult to talk to about this**. I have no memory of even considering speaking to my parents. Who else? I had met Mark Kain and [Craig and Bonnie Housel](https://obits.pennlive.com/us/obituaries/pennlive/name/mabel-housel-obituary?id=14976768), Palm Lutheran's youth leaders, but it was only after the fall out that I really started going to Palm's youth group. I just didn't even seek these relationships with adults. Coaches, teachers. Did I avoid them? Maybe. Maybe I learned from my experience in my family to trust myself over adults. Self-protection but also self-confidence, in a way. Maybe because of those friendships with peers, I... Well, I was going to say I didn't need them until then, but the shit in my family is overwhelming counter-evidence.

I was just a scared kid. Scared at home, at church, at school.

Pastor Bair became special to me for a reason. My first adult. No, not fair. My second, really. Mark Kain, but I did never risk opening up to Mark. I just soaked in Mark's affirmation and attention. Pastor Bair was the first I opened up to. 

Is that who Marie is to me now?

Sadness. A primal loneliness. Who do I open up to now? No one, really. Fully known?
