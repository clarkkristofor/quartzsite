This morning I've been gazing at the tree in my neighbor's backyard and marveling at the branches and the way they grew. The branches tell a story of history and context - weather and storms, the sun's movement and position, other trees or their absence. This tree is not a self made tree. It's responding to its environment. Its environment shapes it. The same is true of me, of people.
