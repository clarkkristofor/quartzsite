Abby dream last night. At Hershey Park. Watching a concert with friends. Troy and another young man. Cornell was about to restart. Abby and I were cuddling. Like I was her boyfriend pillow. She was sleepy. Felt so right and good and intimate. The concert ended and our bill came for our dinner purchases. They gave it to me to do the math of who owed what. I turned away from Abby and she turned away from me to talk to the other young man. I felt jealous, left out, like the sun had gone away. I was eavesdropping on their conversation. He and Abby weren't going straight back to school. Too much was similar in their plans, not that they were doing them together but I envied their closeness and regretted my distance. They said something about concerts. In a brash way, I said, "We just saw Radiohead. How great is that?" I kept struggling with the math. I couldn't concentrate even though it was simple arithmetic. Troy and I stood talking about his drink, which was yummy. We joked that the smell of his breath now would attract women. "What is that wonderful smell?" I woke up struggling with the math and yet with the compulsion to stay asleep to finish it.
