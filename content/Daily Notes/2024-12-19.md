2 am can't sleep
Part of me is simply afraid to be awake in the night. Doesn't like to be reminded of
- that night drawing is be left behind in the night, praying that Jesus would come into my heart, feeling nothing and praying again
- a night fearing annihilation by God 
- that night seeing a shadow and thinking it was Dad standing outside my room watching, I stared at the shadow terrified for so long. I realized in time it was the shadow of a train on my tall dresser 
- a night fearing my dad 
- that night terrified of "6 5 6 5 6 5..." I repeated it compulsively and what emerged, the way the words became just patterns of sound, terrified me, I don't know why
This is not simply fear of the dark. It's the nighttime amplification of the daylight chatter, the background noise of my warring parts.

These are not childish, childlike fear of no future, of no safety, of no escape, of utter aloneness and isolation, no possibility of trust, friendship, comfort.

Worthless? Which is life or death. Says Schwartz. Worthlessness doesn't resonate but the existential threat does. Awake at night is that threat, unleased 

Plus the nattering manager insisting I sleep, catastrophizing about a tired tomorrow. Afraid the exile will bring down the system. Firefighter bringing compulsion in prayer, empty in a way, unsatisfying because it's pleading with my tormentor, screaming into the void
