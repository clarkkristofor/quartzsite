> The word “hope” has many definitions, but as my dear friend, the actor Letitia Wright, wonderfully phrases it via spoken-word interludes on our new album, *[Here Because of Hope,](https://ezracollective.com/pages/here-because-of-hope)* hope also captures the belief that on the other side of pain there can be renewal. There’s joy after mourning. Beauty from the ashes. Even when you don’t know how or when things will change, there can still be something inside you that believes your feet will eventually land on solid ground.
>
 > –**Femi Koleoso**, British-Nigerian drummer in the [Ezra Collective](https://ezracollective.com/)
