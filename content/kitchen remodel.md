### Build (8-12 hrs, $600)
- **Day 1:** Pick up plywood.
- **Day 2 Morning (3–4 hrs):** Break down plywood sheets into simple box panels. Pre-drill 2.5" plumbing and 2" dishwasher utility holes while the panels are flat.
- **Day 2 Afternoon (2–3 hrs):** Iron on  pre-glued edgebanding (Paint-Grade Paper or Wood Veneer) to cover raw plywood edges, then trim them flush.
- **Day 3 (3–5 hrs):** Screw the boxes together using pocket holes or pilot screws, and attach the back panels.
### Finish (3 days)
- **Day 4:** **Sand** everything smooth, wipe away the dust, and apply a high-quality primer.
- **Day 5:** Lightly sand the primer, then apply your **first coat** of cabinet paint.
- **Day 6:** Apply your **second and final coat of paint**. 5-7 days to harden.
## Demo, Plumbing & Install

- **Demo & Clean:** Remove old cabinets and counters down to bare walls and subfloor.
- **Paint walls:** first coat

- **Plumbing Rough-In (~3 hrs, $600–$800):** Pro plumber drills the floor and runs the new PVC lines and water valves up from the basement.

- **Install Countertop:**
- **Install Dishwasher:** Slide the 18-inch dishwasher into its bay, level it, and route the lines horizontally into the sink cabinet cavity _before_ the top goes on.

# Cabinet Hole Guide
**Through the Cabinet Base (For the Plumber):**
- **Main Drain:** **2.5-inch hole** (leaves ideal clearance for standard $1\frac{1}{2}\text{"}$ or $2\text{"}$ PVC).
- **Water Supplies (Hot & Cold):** **1.25-inch holes** (two separate holes spaced 4 to 6 inches apart for $\frac{1}{2}\text{"}$ supply lines).

**Through the Side Wall (To the Dishwasher):**
- **Hoses (Drain & Supply):** **2-inch hole** tucked near the bottom back corner to route both flexible lines together.
- **Power Cord:** **2-inch hole** placed slightly higher up to allow a standard three-prong electrical plug to pass through flat.

# Plumbing Plan
- **The Strategy:** Avoid the risky cast-iron wall stack. The pro plumber will drop the new sink drain straight down through the floor into the basement and tie it into the main stack just 2 feet away.
- **Cost & Time:** Expect **$600 to $800** and roughly **2 to 3 hours** of labor from a professional.
- **The Disposal:** Replaced with a premium multi-stage unit (like an InSinkErator Evolution) to protect old pipes and feed Davenport’s green energy anaerobic digesters.
