# MORTA!
*Cultist of Morta* at Gamicon, 2025-03-01

A fast silly storytelling party game about cultists and their demon lord, [Morta](https://smalltowngames.games/shop/p/morta). By the same designers as [[surprisingly thinky|Magical Mushroom Mayhem]]. The co-designer taught us the game. The kids loved it!

5 stars.
