# Christmas Betrayal
*Betrayal at the House on the Hill*, 2024-12-24

A: Peter Akimoto
SJ: Heather Granville
SK: Ox Bellows
C: Madame Zostra

The Haunt was revealed after the third Omen. Early!

![[PXL_20241224_163358414.jpg]]

Peter betrayed us! He let blood sucking bats into the house. The bats killed him and then tried to kill us.

We rushed to the Basement Organ room. Madame Zostra and her Madman companion started the organ. Bats searched Heather in the Foyer! But she fought then off. Then time after time, we tried to figure out what combination of organ blasts would drive the bats away. Not even Heather, the smartest of us, could do it. 

Then we discovered the Catacombs and bats flooded through it into the Organ room! They killed Heather, before she could remember to apply her Healing Salve.

Finally Madame Zostra and her Madman companion mashed the organ keyboard! The blast scattered the bats and drove them away! Zostra and the Madman killed the last two bats, which had clung to her and Ox.

Two of our friends were dead. But two survived. A victory, of sorts.
