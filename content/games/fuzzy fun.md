# fuzzy fun
*The Fuzzies* at Gamicon, 2025-03-01

A young host, maybe 13 years old, taught us this game. We'd previously seen it [reviewed by Shut Up and Sit Down](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Yx2bFbdUJ7M)--a Jenga-like dexterity game with fuzzy balls instead of wood blocks. We played several fast rounds in roughly 30 minutes and had a great time.

5 stars. The kids want it.
