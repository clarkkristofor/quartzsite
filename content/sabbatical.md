## Part 1: London, from SE1

### Getting around from the Airbnb

The Airbnb sits in one of the best-connected, most walkable parts of London.

|Nearby|Walk from Airbnb|Useful for|
|---|---|---|
|**Borough station**|~8 min|Northern line (Bank branch): Angel, King's Cross, Euston|
|**London Bridge station**|~10 min|Jubilee line (Westminster, Green Park, Bond Street, Baker Street, Waterloo), Northern line, Thameslink trains|
|[Borough Market](https://boroughmarket.org.uk/) / [Southwark Cathedral](https://cathedral.southwark.anglican.org/)|~10 min|Food, South Bank walks|
|[Fashion and Textile Museum](https://fashiontextilemuseum.org/), Bermondsey Street|short walk|Wife and daughter, Jun 4–5|
|[Tower Bridge](https://www.towerbridge.org.uk/) → [Tower of London](https://www.hrp.org.uk/tower-of-london/)|~20–25 min|Bank holiday Monday|
|[Tate Modern](https://www.tate.org.uk/visit/tate-modern) / [Globe](https://www.shakespearesglobe.com/) / Millennium Bridge|~25 min along the river|Wednesday|

**Key routes for the trip:**

- **Paddington** (Oxford and Bath trains, and the Jun 10 hotel): Jubilee line to Bond Street, then the Elizabeth line one stop to Paddington, about 25 minutes.
- **Euston** (Studio Tour): Northern line from Borough or London Bridge, about 10 minutes, no change.
- **Baker Street** (Daunt Books, Regent's Park): Jubilee line direct from London Bridge, about 15 minutes.
- **Green Park** (the Ritz, Fortnum & Mason, Buckingham Palace): Jubilee line direct from London Bridge, 3 stops.

**Paying:**

- Tap a contactless card or phone at the gates on tubes, buses, and trains. Fares cap automatically each day.
- **Each person needs their own card or device.** You can't tap one card for several people.
- Children under 11 generally travel free with a paying adult. Confirm the current TfL rules for your child's age.

**Apps and checks:**

- Citymapper or Google Maps handle routing well.
- Check [tfl.gov.uk/status-updates](https://tfl.gov.uk/status-updates) for weekend closures before Saturday and Sunday.

**Heat:** The tube gets hot. On warm days, walk or take buses for short hops.

**Shows:** The show on Tue Jun 1 is booked ahead. Any other West End show is spontaneous: check the [TKTS booth](https://www.tkts.co.uk/) in Leicester Square on the day for discounts.

- **After the Tower:** Walk about 10 minutes to [Leadenhall Market](https://www.leadenhallmarket.co.uk/), the Victorian covered market used as a Diagon Alley location in the Harry Potter films. Some shops may be shut on the bank holiday, but the architecture is the point.

### Tue Jun 1 — Bloomsbury bookshops and the West End

- **5:30 — [Kricket](https://kricket.co.uk/soho/)**, 12 Denman Street, two minutes from [Piccadilly Circus](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Piccadilly_Circus). It's a Bib Gourmand Indian restaurant that does pre-theatre dinner and takes walk-ins. Kids eat free during school holidays, and English half-term may fall this week.

- **After:** [The Lyric](https://lyricsoho.co.uk/), a small Victorian pub on Great Windmill Street, if anyone's still upright.

- [Shakespeare's Globe](https://www.shakespearesglobe.com/): Take a daytime tour, or come back for an evening performance.
- If you have more energy, keep walking west to the [Southbank Centre](https://www.southbankcentre.co.uk/), [National Theatre](https://www.nationaltheatre.org.uk/), and [BFI](https://www.bfi.org.uk/).
- **Evening:** An evening at the Globe, or an optional West End show (TKTS on the day).
- Walk or take the Jubilee line home.

### Thu Jun 3 — Oxford day trip

- **Morning:** Take the Jubilee line to Bond Street, then the Elizabeth line to **Paddington**, about 25 minutes. A direct train to Oxford takes about an hour, so you'd arrive around 10:30. The flat stays booked, so only Clark and son carry backpacks for Birmingham. Luggage storage near Oxford station is optional.
- **Late morning:** [Christ Church](https://www.chch.ox.ac.uk/) and its Great Hall. Book timed tickets, and check the date, because the Hall sometimes closes around lunchtime for college use.
- **Lunch**, then the [Bodleian's Divinity School](https://visit.bodleian.ox.ac.uk/) (another Harry Potter filming spot) and the Radcliffe Camera, all a few minutes apart.

An [Inklings](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Inklings) walking tour gives you the Lewis and Tolkien side without leaving the center. [The Kilns](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Kilns) and Tolkien's grave are both out in the suburbs and won't fit into a day like this.
